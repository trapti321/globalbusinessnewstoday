MiloMagazine.co.uk Review: 979 Posts in 13 Months and Guest Posts for $75

Is milomagazine.co.uk a real magazine? We checked its 2025 UK domain, one-byline output, $75 guest post listing and an FDA-flagged supplement post.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 9, 2026 • 6 min read
Share

MiloMagazine.co.uk, often searched as "milomagazine.co.uk usa", is a UK-registered WordPress blog launched in October 2025 that has published almost 1,000 posts in about 13 months, nearly all under one byline. It started with celebrity "husband, age, net worth" articles and now publishes commercial posts that link to law firms, clothing sites and a supplement seller. Its guest posts are advertised for sale on a freelance marketplace.

MiloMagazine.co.uk at a glance:

  • Domain: registered October 16, 2025, according to the UK's Nominet registry.
  • Posts: 979, almost all credited to "Sandra Conway".
  • Pages: only a Contact page. There is no About page.
  • Guest posts: offered for $75 on PeoplePerHour by a third-party seller.
  • Verdict: a high-volume link-selling blog. It is not a UK or US magazine.

What Is MiloMagazine.co.uk?

The site describes itself as delivering "excellent information about your favorite celebrities". Its menu covers Business, Real Estate, Technology, Education, News, Sports, Entertainment, Fashion and Food. A November 2025 snapshot shows its early focus: posts such as "Where Does Rob Beckett Live? Children, Wife Illness".

The ".co.uk" ending means it is a UK domain. There is no separate US edition. The "usa" in searches likely comes from pages the site publishes about other domains with "USA" in the title, such as "USATop.co.uk USA: A Complete Guide".

Who Writes MiloMagazine.co.uk?

The site's WordPress data lists two authors: "Sandra Conway", credited with 978 of 979 posts, and "Leonardo Taylor", with one. Nearly 1,000 posts in 13 months from one byline, across celebrity gossip, law, health, crypto and home improvement, is not a pattern of genuine expert authorship.

The Contact page lists an email on seolinkers.com, an SEO link-building domain. The same domain appears as the content contribution address on AelfTech.com.

What Does It Publish?

Recent posts link to businesses with followed links and no sponsored label:

  • "Arrested in Florida? What Happens Next and How to Protect Yourself", linking to a criminal defence firm
  • "How Does Raspberry Hills Clothing Fit Into Modern Streetwear", linking twice to a clothing site
  • "How Ostarine Supports Muscle Recovery: A Guide to Its Medical Potential" (November 6, 2026), linking to a seller with the anchor "Ostarine Australia"
  • Crypto and forex platform reviews, and a post on account safety for a gambling brand

The ostarine post is a concern. Ostarine is a selective androgen receptor modulator (SARM). The US Food and Drug Administration warns that SARMs are unapproved drugs that cannot be legally marketed as supplements, and that they have been linked to liver injury, heart attack and stroke risks.

MiloMagazine.co.uk Guest Posts for Sale

A PeoplePerHour listing offers a "Guest Post On Milomagazine.co.uk" for $75, delivered in three days, with a "do-follow backlink" and "permanent backlink placement". The seller claims a Domain Authority of 41 and "44.8K+" monthly traffic. These are the seller's figures, which we could not verify. Google's spam policies treat buying links to manipulate rankings as link spam.

The site does not ask readers for money. It is a link-selling blog, and its health, legal and finance content should not be trusted. For other sites in this network, see our reviews of Tozymagazine.com and MagazinePanda.com.

MiloMagazine.co.uk FAQs

What is milomagazine.co.uk usa?

There is no US edition. MiloMagazine.co.uk is a UK-registered blog, and "usa" appears in some of its page titles about other websites.

Who runs MiloMagazine.co.uk?

The site names no owner, the domain registration is private, and its contact email is on an SEO link-building domain.

Can you buy a guest post on MiloMagazine.co.uk?

A third-party seller lists one for $75. We do not recommend it.
