Washington Nationals vs Chicago Cubs Match Player Stats: Cavalli's One-Hitter in a 7-0 Win

The Washington Nationals beat the Chicago Cubs 7-0 on Thursday, August 13, 2026, at Nationals Park, as Cade Cavalli took a no-hitter into the seventh inning. It was the most recent Nationals vs Cubs game. Below are the full match player stats for pitchers and hitters from the ESPN box score, checked against the official MLB Stats API.

Nationals vs Cubs at a glance: Nationals 7, Cubs 0 · August 13, 2026 · Nationals Park, Washington · Attendance 21,463 · Time 2:20. Winning pitcher: Cade Cavalli (8 IP, 1 H, 10 K). Losing pitcher: Kevin Gausman. Key hitter: Abimelec Ortiz (2-for-4, three-run home run).

Cavalli retired the first 15 batters he faced. Chicago's only hit came from Michael Busch with two outs in the seventh inning. The win let Washington avoid a three-game sweep, though Chicago took the series 2-1. It is part of our match player stats series, alongside Texans vs Steelers and Celtics vs Cavaliers.
