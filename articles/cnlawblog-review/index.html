CNLawBlog Review: Is Cnlawblog.com a Real Law Blog or a Guest Post Site?

Is cnlawblog a real law blog? We checked cnlawblog.com's empty law sections, dofollow guest post offer, authors, history and its China Law Blog confusion.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 8, 2026 • 7 min read
Share

CNLawBlog (cnlawblog.com) sounds like a law blog, but most of what it publishes is general business, tech, finance and local-service content, and its Guest Post page advertises "dofollow backlinks". It is not the well-known China Law Blog from the law firm Harris Sliwoski. We checked cnlawblog.com's domain history, authors, links and pages on November 8, 2026. It belongs to a group of sites we reviewed that share contacts and bylines, including Puzutask.com.

CNLawBlog.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a multi-topic WordPress blog that calls itself a "Legal, Business, Finance & Tech Blog", with 203 posts.
  • Since: current site founded August 2018, though the domain had earlier owners.
  • Law content: 19 posts in the Law category. Every law sub-category (Criminal, Family, Corporate and so on) is empty.
  • Guest posts: accepted, with "1–2 dofollow backlinks". Price on request.
  • Verdict: not a legal resource. Do not rely on it for legal advice.

What Is CNLawBlog?

The site's title is "CNLawBlog.com: Legal, Business, Finance & Tech Blog". Its menu lists Technology, Finance, Business, Education, Health, Lifestyle, Real Estate and Law. Law has sub-categories for Accident, Civil, Corporate, Criminal, Family and Personal Injury, plus Travel, Automotive, Fashion, Politics and Sports. Its About page says it was founded in August 2018.

Despite the name, law is a minor topic. The largest categories are Business (32 posts), General (31) and Technology (25), against 19 for Law. Every law sub-category is empty.

Is CNLawBlog the Same as China Law Blog?

No. China Law Blog is published by the US law firm Harris Sliwoski LLP and covers legal issues for companies doing business in China. Cnlawblog.com has no connection to it, and its homepage and About page never mention China. Cnlawblog.com's own FAQ says it "is not affiliated with similarly named websites." Lookalike domains such as cnlawblog.it.com also exist.

Domain History

  • Registration: the official Verisign registry record shows the current registration began on August 11, 2018, through NameCheap, and runs to 2033. You can check it with the ICANN registration lookup.
  • Earlier use: the Wayback Machine has captures from 2005. A 2016 snapshot shows a generic legal-services blog.
  • 2019: by February 2019 the site's title read "Cnlawblog.com | Guest Blog | Submit Guest blog – Write For US".

The site's first post, dated August 13, 2018, is "URDU CALLIGRAPHY ON YOUR WALLS". Its newest is dated August 25, 2026.

Does CNLawBlog Sell Guest Posts?

Its Guest Post page promises contributors "high-quality dofollow backlinks" that "improve your website's SEO and domain authority". It allows "1–2 dofollow backlinks". On price, it answers "Is guest posting on CNLawBlog free?" with "It depends… Contact us for details." Google's spam policies treat links bought to manipulate rankings as link spam.

Commercial links we checked, to a home remodeler, a lending app and a business lender, were plain followed links with no sponsored label. Many posts dated February 9, 2026 target local services in US towns, such as roofing in Libertyville, flooring in Mount Prospect and pest control in Lansdale.

Who Runs CNLawBlog?

The site names no owner or editor. Its four author accounts, "Jamie", "mansi", "Ompal Rao" and "Roland", have no biographies or legal credentials. Guest-post submissions go to a Gmail address. That same address appears on SparkPressFusion.com and Yonosamachar.com. Three of the author names also appear on Puzutask.com, and both sites show the same banner links. These shared details suggest a common publishing network, though we could not confirm ownership.

Cnlawblog.com also publishes reviews of itself, such as "CNLawBlog Review: Features, Content Quality, and Is It Worth Using?" (March 20, 2026).

What Others Say

A StartupEditor review gave cnlawblog.com a "45/100, use with caution" trust score. It noted that no named operator is identified, and found no evidence that the site is a government, court, bar or law firm site. StartupRise called it "an independent blog, not an official or government-run platform" and found no major scam complaints.

Is CNLawBlog Legit?

It is a real, active blog and we found no scam reports. But it is not a legal resource: its writers have no listed qualifications, its law sections are empty, and it openly sells dofollow links. For legal questions, use a licensed lawyer, your local bar association's referral service, or official court and government websites.

CNLawBlog FAQs

What is cnlawblog?

A multi-topic WordPress blog at cnlawblog.com, founded in 2018, covering business, tech, finance and some legal topics, and accepting guest posts.

Is cnlawblog run by lawyers?

Not as far as we can tell. No author lists legal qualifications, and the site names no law firm.

Is cnlawblog connected to China Law Blog?

No. China Law Blog is published by Harris Sliwoski LLP at harris-sliwoski.com.

Information was checked on November 8, 2026, using the Verisign registry record, the Wayback Machine, the site's own pages and WordPress data, and the third-party reviews linked above. This article is not legal advice. We will update it if CNLawBlog responds or changes.
