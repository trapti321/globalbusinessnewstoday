Puzutask.com Review: It Is Not a Task or Earning Platform. Here Is What It Is

Can you earn money on puzutask.com? No. We checked the site: a 2021 WordPress blog with casino posts, disabled sign-ups and no tasks or payouts.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 8, 2026 • 6 min read
Share

Puzutask.com is not a task-management app or an online earning platform, despite how several websites describe it. It is a multi-topic WordPress blog that publishes technology, finance, health and lifestyle posts, along with casino and sports betting content. We checked the site, its domain history and its features on November 8, 2026. It shares authors and banners with CNLawBlog, which we reviewed the same day.

Puzutask.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a WordPress blog with 364 posts, the newest dated October 19, 2026.
  • Tasks or earnings: none. There are no task, wallet, deposit or payout features, and public sign-up is disabled.
  • Domain: registered March 15, 2021.
  • Authors: eight bylines with no bios.
  • Verdict: a general content blog. Ignore any site or message that presents Puzutask as a way to earn money.

What Is Puzutask.com?

The site's title is "Puzutask.com | Digital Publication Covering Tech, AI, Finance & More". Its About page calls it "a multi-category digital publication" covering technology, artificial intelligence, finance, business, cryptocurrency and trading. The menu lists Health, Technology, Finance, Fashion, Education, General and Lifestyle.

The homepage's meta description reads: "If you searched for Puzutask com, you have reached the right place." This wording targets people searching for the site's name.

Is Puzutask.com a Task or Earning Platform?

No. Some websites, such as StartupRise, describe Puzutask.com as "a digital task management platform" with "customizable dashboards", or hint that users can earn from tasks. We found none of that on the live site:

  • The "Register" menu item points to the WordPress admin login, and public registration is switched off.
  • There are no task lists, dashboards, wallets, deposits or payouts.
  • The site has no Contact or Write for Us page, and it does not ask visitors for money or personal details.

Puzutask's own September 2026 self-review agrees. It says the name may make people think of "an online earning platform" but that the site is "a multi-topic content and publishing website". If anyone asks you to pay a fee or deposit to "unlock tasks" on Puzutask, treat it as a scam. Task scams of this kind are a known fraud pattern, as the US Federal Trade Commission warns.

Domain History

  • Registration: the official Verisign registry record shows puzutask.com was registered on March 15, 2021, through Wild West Domains. You can check it with the ICANN registration lookup.
  • 2021: the first Wayback Machine snapshot, from September 2021, shows "Puzu Task – Saving the world", already a multi-topic blog.
  • 2023: by December 2023 the title was "All things are possible With Puzutask", and the site called itself "a leading News Portal".

What Does Puzutask.com Publish?

Its 364 posts run from May 2021 to October 2026. The largest categories are "Top Ranked" (175 posts) and "Top Post" (46). Recent headlines include:

  • "How Offshore Staffing Helps Small Businesses Scale in a Competitive Global Market" (October 19, 2026)
  • "Building a Strong Digital Brand Presence for Online Casinos" (September 4, 2026)
  • "How Sports Betting Platforms Became Full Entertainment Ecosystems" (August 5, 2026)
  • "Summer Nail Designs 2026: Cute, Colorful & Stylish Ideas" (September 3, 2026)

Gambling posts link to betting sites with plain followed links, for example a casino slots page with the anchor "casino slots". The sidebar promotes hemp wraps. We saw no sponsored labels on commercial links.

Who Runs Puzutask.com?

The site names no owner. Its About page lists a Gmail address, and its eight author accounts have no bios. Three of those names, "mansi", "Ompal Rao" and "Roland", also write for CNLawBlog.com, and both sites carry the same banner links. That points to a shared publishing network, but we could not confirm who owns it.

Puzutask.com FAQs

What is puzutask.com?

A multi-topic WordPress blog registered in 2021. It is not a task app or earning platform.

Can I make money on Puzutask.com?

No. The site has no tasks, wallets or payouts. Be wary of anyone who says otherwise.

How do I sign up for Puzutask?

You can't. Public registration is disabled, and the "Register" link leads to the site's admin login.
