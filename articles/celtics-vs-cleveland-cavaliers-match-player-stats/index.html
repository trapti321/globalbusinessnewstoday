Celtics vs Cleveland Cavaliers Match Player Stats: Box Score From Boston's 109-98 Win

Celtics vs Cavaliers match player stats from Boston's 109-98 win on March 8, 2026: Mitchell 30, Mobley 24, Brown 23, Tatum 20, plus every recent result.

Share

The Boston Celtics beat the Cleveland Cavaliers 109-98 on Sunday, March 8, 2026, at Rocket Arena in Cleveland. It was the most recent regular-season Celtics vs Cavaliers game, and it completed Boston's 3-0 season sweep. Below are the full match player stats and team totals from the ESPN box score.

Celtics vs Cavaliers at a glance: Celtics 109, Cavaliers 98 · March 8, 2026 · Rocket Arena, Cleveland · Attendance 19,432. Top scorers: Donovan Mitchell 30 (CLE), Evan Mobley 24 (CLE), Jaylen Brown 23 (BOS), Jayson Tatum 20 (BOS).

It was Jayson Tatum's second game back from a torn right Achilles tendon. Boston built a 56-36 halftime lead and held off a Cleveland comeback in the second half.

What the Stats Show

The game turned in the second quarter, when Boston held Cleveland to 10 points. The Cavaliers outscored the Celtics 62-53 after halftime, but went 13 of 45 from three and could not close the gap. Boston's 20 of 22 free throws and seven-rebound edge also helped.

The win moved Boston to 43-21, within three games of the East-leading Detroit Pistons (see our Pistons vs Magic player stats), and dropped Cleveland to 39-25. It also ended the Cavaliers' seven-game home winning streak.
