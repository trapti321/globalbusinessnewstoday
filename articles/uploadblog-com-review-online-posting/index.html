UploadBlog.com Review: How Online Posting Works, Dofollow Link Rules and What We Verified

UploadBlog.com is a guest-post blog offering one do-follow link per article. We checked its 195 posts, submission rules, link labelling and domain history.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 2, 2026 •Updated Nov 11, 2026 • 7 min read
Share

UploadBlog.com is a general-interest WordPress blog that accepts guest posts and openly offers contributors "one do-follow link" to their own website. It is not a self-service publishing platform where you can create an account and upload posts yourself: you email a draft and the site decides whether to publish it. We checked the site's pages, its public post data and its domain records on November 11, 2026, after it blocked our first check on November 2.

UploadBlog.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a WordPress blog with 195 posts on technology, AI tools, solar energy, health and home topics.
  • How posting works: email a pitch or finished article to the site's admin address. There is no sign-up or upload tool.
  • Links: its Write for Us page allows "One do-follow link" and promises "high-quality backlinks". None of the 54 outbound links in its latest 100 posts is labelled sponsored or nofollow.
  • Domain: current registration dates from August 12, 2025. The name was used by unrelated sites between 2007 and 2014.
  • Verdict: a guest-post blog. Fine for occasional exposure, but not a reliable SEO strategy.

What Is UploadBlog.com?

The homepage of UploadBlog.com calls it a "Trusted Knowledge Magazine" offering "Insights, Reviews & Guides That Help You Make Better Decisions". Its About page describes "a modern, general blogging platform" for readers "in Australia, the USA, and beyond".

In practice it is a WordPress blog. On November 11, 2026, its public data listed 195 posts. Its largest categories are "Blog" (72 posts), "UploadBlog" (53), Technology & Gadgets (21), Solar Energy (20) and Health Tips (16). Much of the content targets Australian searches, such as solar feed-in tariffs in Victoria, a mortgage broker in Sydney Olympic Park and video production in Melbourne.

How Does Online Posting on UploadBlog.com Work?

Many third-party guides describe UploadBlog as a platform where anyone can "upload" a blog post. That is not how the site works. Its Write for Us page asks contributors to email "your article pitch or completed draft" with a short bio and links. Its stated rules are:

  • Articles must be "100% unique and not published elsewhere".
  • Length: "Minimum 800–1500 words".
  • Links: "One do-follow link to your website or portfolio is allowed".
  • Images: copyright-free, or the site can help.

The page does not mention a fee, and it does not say how long review takes. Besides the admin email address on the site's own domain, the page lists a WhatsApp number with a United Arab Emirates (+971) country code, while the contact page gives the site's locations as "Sydney, Australia | Los Angeles, USA". Before paying anyone for a placement, ask for written terms and an invoice.

Domain history

According to the Verisign .com registry, the current registration of uploadblog.com began on August 12, 2025, through the registrar Spaceship, Inc., and runs to August 12, 2027. The name itself is much older: the Wayback Machine has snapshots going back to 2007, when it was used by unrelated sites. A previously used domain name can carry old backlinks, which is one reason such names are re-registered for new blogs.

Why our first check failed

On November 2, 2026, the site returned "403 Forbidden" to our requests, and one service was redirected to a page on recaptcha.world, a domain unrelated to Google's reCAPTCHA. On November 11 the site loaded normally. Bot protection settings change, so access may vary by network.

Does Posting on UploadBlog.com Help SEO?

Probably not much, and it carries some risk. Google's spam policies list "links with optimized anchor text in articles, guest posts, or press releases distributed on other sites" as an example of link spam. Google also says paid links are fine "as long as they are qualified with a rel="nofollow" or rel="sponsored" attribute".

UploadBlog's offer of a do-follow link in exchange for a guest post, with no link labelling across its recent posts, is exactly the kind of placement Google's systems are designed to discount. A post there may bring a few readers, but you should not expect it to lift your rankings.

If you publish on any guest site, measure results in your own analytics (referral visits and enquiries) rather than relying on promised backlink value.

Similar Sites We Have Reviewed

UploadBlog is one of many general blogs built around guest posts. Our reviews of SparkPressFusion.com, MagazinePanda.com and Adstotally.com found similar patterns: self-referential posts, unlabelled commercial links and little information about who runs the site. Unlike several of those sites, UploadBlog uses a contact address on its own domain rather than a shared Gmail account.

How to Check Any Guest Post Site Before You Submit

  1. Confirm the official domain. Look-alike names such as uploadblog.org or uploadblog.us are separate sites.
  2. Check who you are emailing. Prefer contact addresses on the publication's own domain.
  3. Get the rules in writing: fees, review time, editing rights, removal requests and link labelling.
  4. Look up the domain age in a free registry lookup, and check its archive history.
  5. Check indexing with a site:domain.com search.
  6. Never pay before you see terms and an invoice from the publication itself.
  7. Publish the original on your own site first, or keep a dated copy.

Alternatives to UploadBlog.com

For most small businesses, a blog on your own website keeps the content and traffic under your name. LinkedIn articles, Medium and industry trade publications reach established audiences. For genuine company news, Global Business News Today accepts announcements through its press release submission form, and paid releases are clearly labelled.

UploadBlog.com FAQs

Is UploadBlog.com free to post on?

Its Write for Us page does not mention a fee. Confirm in writing before submitting, and never pay without an invoice.

Can I upload a blog post myself on UploadBlog.com?

No. There is no account or upload tool. You email your draft to the site.

Does UploadBlog.com give do-follow links?

Its rules allow "One do-follow link". Google advises that links placed in exchange for content should be labelled sponsored or nofollow.

Who owns UploadBlog.com?

The site does not name an owner. It lists locations in Sydney and Los Angeles and a UAE WhatsApp number.

Is UploadBlog.com safe?

We found no malware or scam reports, and reading it is low-risk. Treat it as a guest-post blog, not an authoritative source.
