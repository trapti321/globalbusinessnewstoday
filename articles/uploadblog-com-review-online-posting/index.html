UploadBlog.com Review: What We Could Verify About Online Posting Before You Submit

We checked UploadBlog.com's domain records, access and the reviews ranking for it. Here is what is verified, what is not, and how to vet any guest post site.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 2, 2026 • 6 min read
Share

UploadBlog.com is a website that is widely described online as a place to publish articles and guest posts, but very little about how its online posting works can be confirmed from the site itself. When we checked on November 2, 2026, UploadBlog.com blocked our automated requests, and the reviews that rank for its name contradict each other on basic points such as fees, review times and link rules.

If you run a small business and are weighing UploadBlog.com for brand exposure or backlinks, this review sets out what we could verify, what we could not, and a checklist you can use on any guest posting site before you submit an article.

What Is UploadBlog.com?

UploadBlog.com is presented in third-party guides as a multi-topic blogging site that publishes articles on subjects such as technology, business, health and travel, and that accepts contributions from outside writers. Those descriptions come from other websites. We were not able to load the site's own pages to confirm its categories, submission process or policies.

The domain is recent. According to the official .com registry record from Verisign, uploadblog.com was registered on August 12, 2025, through the registrar Spaceship, Inc., and is registered until August 12, 2027.

What We Found When We Tried to Check the Site

  • Access blocked: Requests to uploadblog.com from our network returned an HTTP 403 "Forbidden" error, whether or not they identified as a standard web browser.
  • Redirect to another domain: A request from a separate web service was redirected to a page on recaptcha.world, a different domain. That domain is not Google's reCAPTCHA service, which runs on google.com. We did not follow the redirect.
  • Few indexed pages from the site itself: A search for pages on the uploadblog.com domain mostly returned articles about UploadBlog hosted on other websites, rather than pages from the site.

None of this proves the site is unsafe. Many sites use bot protection, and access can vary by country and network. It does mean that anyone planning to publish there should confirm the site's rules directly, in writing, rather than relying on secondhand reviews.

Why UploadBlog Reviews Disagree

Most search results for "uploadblog.com online posting" are reviews and "write for us" pages published on other domains, several using similar names such as uploadblog.org, uploadblog.us and uploadrblog.com. We found no statement on those pages confirming that they are run by the owner of uploadblog.com.

The claims vary. A "write for us" page on uploadblog.org says submissions are free, reviewed in about 1 to 7 days, and may include one or two links, but its listed contact address is on a third domain, linkifymedia.com. A review on uploadflow.io says there is "no clear published policy on what gets accepted, what gets rejected, or how long review takes," and notes that it is based on publicly available information rather than a test submission.

Because these pages contradict each other and none shows a documented test, we treat claims about UploadBlog.com's fees, approval times and link policies as unverified.

Does Posting on UploadBlog.com Help SEO?

There is no public evidence we could find that a post on UploadBlog.com improves search rankings, and Google's rules limit what guest posts can do. Google's spam policies list "links with optimized anchor text in articles, guest posts, or press releases distributed on other sites" as an example of link spam.

Google also says paid links are not a violation "as long as they are qualified with a rel="nofollow" or rel="sponsored" attribute." In practice, a guest post placed mainly to gain a keyword-rich link carries ranking risk, while a post that earns readers or referral traffic on its own merits does not.

If you do publish on any guest site, judge it on referral visits and enquiries, which you can measure in your own analytics, rather than on promised backlink value.

How to Check Any Guest Post Site Before You Submit

  1. Confirm the official domain. Make sure you are dealing with the site you intend to publish on, not a similarly named domain.
  2. Check who you are emailing. Contact addresses should be on the publication's own domain. Ask for written terms if they are not.
  3. Get the rules in writing. Ask about fees, review times, editing rights, removal requests and whether links are marked nofollow or sponsored.
  4. Look up the domain age. Free registry lookups show when a domain was created. A site registered recently has less history to judge.
  5. Check that its pages are indexed. Search for site:domain.com. If few of its own pages appear, a post there may not be found in search.
  6. Never pay before you see terms. Avoid sending money for placement without a written agreement and an invoice from the publication itself.
  7. Keep your own copy. Publish the original on your own website first or keep a dated copy, so you do not lose the work if a post is removed.

What Are the Alternatives to UploadBlog.com?

For most small businesses, the safer route is to publish on a platform you control or one with a long public track record. Your own company blog keeps the content and traffic under your name. LinkedIn articles and Medium reach established audiences, and trade publications in your industry offer readers who are already interested in your field.

Press releases are another option for genuine company news. Global Business News Today reviews submitted announcements for accuracy before publication through its press release submission form.

Our Verdict on UploadBlog.com

UploadBlog.com may be a working publication, but as of November 2, 2026, its own pages could not be checked, the domain is just over a year old, and the most visible reviews disagree and are not based on documented tests. Until the site publishes clear contributor terms that you can read and confirm, treat it as an unproven channel: test it with low-stakes content, keep your original, and measure results in your own analytics.

We will update this review if UploadBlog.com publishes official contributor guidelines or responds to our findings. Information checked on November 2, 2026.
