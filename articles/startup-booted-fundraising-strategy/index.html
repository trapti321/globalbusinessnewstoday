Startup Booted Fundraising Strategy Explained, Plus What We Found on StartupBooted.com

What is the Startup Booted fundraising strategy? We explain its 5 bootstrapping steps and check startupbooted.com's authors, $2k service and paid links.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 9, 2026 • 7 min read
Share

Startup Booted (startupbooted.com) is a Wix-built startup blog and consulting site that publishes funding news, "net worth" articles and guest posts, and sells pitch decks, financial models and fundraising strategy services. Its "Startup Booted fundraising strategy" means raising money while staying mostly bootstrapped. Its Write for Us page openly sells dofollow backlinks and says the operator runs "300 high-authority websites." We checked the site on November 9, 2026.

Startup Booted at a glance:

  • What it is: a startup blog with about 1,729 posts, plus a consulting service for pitch decks, financial modelling and fundraising.
  • Domain: registered February 15, 2022, through Wix.
  • Fundraising strategy: revenue-first growth with selective outside capital. The service page says pricing "starts at $2k".
  • Guest posts: sold with "permanent dofollow backlinks", rates on request.
  • Verdict: some useful general advice, but it is a commercial link-selling operation with no named experts.

What Is the Startup Booted Fundraising Strategy?

Startup Booted's fundraising strategy article explains that "booted" is a spelling variant that often appears in searches for "bootstrapped". Its service page defines the approach as "a founder-led approach to raising capital while remaining bootstrapped, focusing on revenue-first growth, selective capital intake, and minimal equity dilution", sitting between traditional bootstrapping and venture capital.

The article sets out five steps:

  1. Validate demand before building. Its line: "A waitlist isn't validation. A payment is."
  2. Launch a revenue-generating minimum viable product.
  3. Reinvest revenue carefully.
  4. Operate lean, with low fixed costs and contractors.
  5. Raise from a position of strength, once revenue gives you negotiating power.

It suggests funding through personal savings, revenue and non-dilutive options such as revenue-based financing, grants, accelerators and customer prepayments. It names metrics to track: monthly recurring revenue growth, customer acquisition cost versus lifetime value, gross margin, net revenue retention above 100% and runway.

Is the Advice Any Good?

The core ideas are mainstream: prove customers will pay, keep costs low and raise outside money when you have leverage. They also fit the article's own warning that bootstrapping suits some businesses better than others. It notes that capital-intensive sectors such as hardware and biotech are a poor fit.

The weaknesses are in the sourcing. The article is credited to "Evelyn Carter" with no credentials. A key example about Ring's founder ends "according to." with the source missing, and a TechCrunch reference has no link. For startup funding, compare it with official resources such as the US Small Business Administration's guide to funding your business.

For more on non-dilutive options, see our reports on the shift to venture debt and fintech tools for SME cash flow.

What Else Does Startup Booted Publish?

The site's sitemap lists about 1,729 blog posts since 2022. Recent topics include startup funding rounds, celebrity "net worth" articles and general guest posts, for example on how transactional lawyers help with business deals. It also publishes casino content, such as "best online casinos in Canada for 2026", with followed links to a casino and no responsible-gambling notice.

Author profiles on the site include "Evelyn Carter" (394 posts) and "Sydney Clarke" (526 posts), both created on March 10, 2026, plus accounts called "startupbooted94776" and "seodelhi2021". The About page claims "a dynamic team of seasoned professionals" but names nobody.

Startup Booted Write for Us: Paid Backlinks

The Write for Us page is explicit:

  • "High-Quality Dofollow Backlinks: Boost your SEO with authority links (1-2 per article)"
  • "Permanent dofollow backlinks (links never removed)" and "Published in 24 Hours"
  • "Link Insertion: Add your link to existing high-traffic, indexed articles"
  • "We operate 300 high-authority websites across different niches"
  • "Standard Guest Post: Contact for current rates"

The page also claims a newsletter "sent to 10,000+ subscribers" and sites with "DA 40-70+". We could not verify any of these claims, and the page ends with an unfilled "Email: [your-email]" placeholder. Google's spam policies treat buying or selling links to manipulate rankings as link spam.

Domain History

  • Registration: the official Verisign registry record shows startupbooted.com was registered on February 15, 2022, through Wix.com, and runs to 2027.
  • 2022: a February 2022 snapshot shows "ONE STOP GUIDE FOR ENTREPRENEURS", described as "a unique blog for entrepreneurs to explore all about startups and growth marketing".
  • Footer: the current site footer says "© 2010 - 2025", although the domain dates from 2022.

Startup Booted FAQs

What does "startup booted" mean?

It refers to startupbooted.com. The site says "booted" is a variant of "bootstrapped".

What is the Startup Booted fundraising strategy?

Grow from customer revenue first, keep costs lean and raise selectively once you have leverage, to limit equity dilution.

Does Startup Booted sell guest posts?

Yes. Its Write for Us page offers dofollow backlinks and link insertions, with rates on request.
