FortMyersWA.com: Not a Fort Myers Utility or Travel Site, but a Parked Former Car Dealer Domain

FortMyersWA.com is not a Fort Myers utility or travel site. It was a used car dealer's website and is now a parked domain. Here is what we found.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 11, 2026 • 4 min read
Share

FortMyersWA.com is not a live website, a Fort Myers city service or a travel guide. It is a parked domain that once belonged to Fort Myers Wholesale Autos, a used car dealer on South Tamiami Trail in Fort Myers, Florida, and the "WA" stands for Wholesale Autos, not Washington or water. When we checked on November 11, 2026, the address returned an error page and its name servers pointed to a domain parking company.

FortMyersWA.com at a glance:

  • What it was: the website of Fort Myers Wholesale Autos, a used car lot at 19561 S. Tamiami Trail, Fort Myers, FL 33908, archived in 2013 and 2014.
  • What it is now: a parked domain. On November 11, 2026, it returned an HTTP 410 "Gone" page and had no HTTPS site.
  • Domain: registered March 18, 2011; current registrar Key-Systems GmbH; name servers at ParkingCrew.
  • Is it a utility or city office? No. For City of Fort Myers water and utility services, use the city's official site.
  • Verdict: there is nothing to buy, pay or log in to at FortMyersWA.com today.

What Is FortMyersWA.com?

On November 11, 2026, a request to FortMyersWA.com returned an HTTP 410 status, which means "gone", with a short error page reading "It is gone! So be gone". A secure HTTPS connection did not load at all. The domain's name servers are ns1 and ns2.parkingcrew.net, which belong to a domain parking service.

There is no inventory, no booking form, no bill payment page and no contact details on the domain. Anyone searching for it today is looking for a website that stopped operating more than a decade ago.

FortMyersWA.com Was a Used Car Dealer's Website

The Wayback Machine at the Internet Archive holds copies of the site from May 2013. Its page title then was "FORT MYERS WHOLESALE AUTOS : Fort Myers car dealer, used cars in Fort Myers, FL". The site listed used vehicles by make, from BMW and Cadillac to Ford and Toyota, and had pages for inventory, financing applications, "Buy Here Pay Here", selling your car and customer testimonials. It listed opening hours of 9:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., Monday to Saturday.

A December 2014 copy gave the dealer's address as 19561 S. Tamiami Trail, Fort Myers, FL 33908, with the slogan "Wholesale to the Public!" and listings such as a 2000 Ford F-350 and a 2001 Infiniti I30. A YouTube video titled "2002 Honda Civic EX - View our current inventory at FortMyersWA.com" also survives from the dealer's listings.

That history explains the name. "WA" is short for Wholesale Autos. It has no connection to Washington state, to a water authority or to any Fort Myers government body.

What Other Articles Get Wrong

The top Google result for "fortmyerswa.com", a page on buzzsport.co.uk, is a general Fort Myers travel guide covering beaches, the Edison and Ford Winter Estates and restaurants. It treats "WA" as stray letters and never says what the domain is. Two other ranking articles, published in June 2026 on pamagazine.co.uk and quicklymagazines.co.uk, describe FortMyersWA.com in the present tense as a platform for used vehicles, which suggests it is still trading online. Archived copies show the dealer site had already been replaced by a parking page by February 2015.

Where to Go Instead

  • City water, sewer and utility services: the City of Fort Myers lists its utilities department at fortmyers.gov/Utilities. The city's official website is fortmyers.gov.
  • Visiting Fort Myers: the official tourism site for the area is VisitFortMyers.com.
  • Buying a used car: check that any dealer holds a current Florida motor vehicle dealer license with the Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles, and run a vehicle history report before you pay.

Is FortMyersWA.com Safe?

There is nothing on the domain to interact with, so visiting it carries little risk. The bigger risk is confusion. Do not send payments, car deposits or personal details to anyone claiming to represent "FortMyersWA.com", because the website has not operated as a dealer since at least 2015. Parked domains can also change hands or start serving ads without notice, so treat any future content there with caution.

FortMyersWA.com FAQs

Is FortMyersWA.com a Fort Myers water or wastewater service?

No. It was a used car dealer's website. City utility services are at fortmyers.gov/Utilities.

What does the "WA" in FortMyersWA.com mean?

Wholesale Autos, from Fort Myers Wholesale Autos, the dealer that used the domain in 2013 and 2014.

Is Fort Myers Wholesale Autos still open?

We could not confirm it. The dealer's website has been offline since at least February 2015, and we found no current official website for the business.

When was FortMyersWA.com registered?

On March 18, 2011, according to the Verisign registry.
