Snapchat Planets Order and Meaning: Full Snapchat Planets List for 2026

The Snapchat planets order from Mercury (1st) to Neptune (8th), what each planet means on a friend's Best Friends (BSF) list, and how to turn it on.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 2, 2026 •Updated Nov 8, 2026 • 7 min read
Share

Snapchat planets are part of the Friend Solar System, a Snapchat+ feature that shows where you sit on a friend's Best Friends list. Your friend is the Sun, and your planet shows how close you are to the top. Mercury is the closest position and Neptune is the eighth.

Snapchat planets order, quick answer: Mercury (1st), Venus (2nd), Earth (3rd), Mars (4th), Jupiter (5th), Saturn (6th), Uranus (7th), Neptune (8th). The closer your planet is to the Sun, the more that friend Snaps and Chats with you. You need Snapchat+ to see it.

Snapchat says the feature reflects how often a friend communicates with you, and that no one else can see it. Below is the full Snapchat planets list and what each planet means. We also cover how to turn the feature on, what "BSF list planets" means, and whether fan sites such as snapchatplanetsorders.com can be trusted.

What Are Snapchat Planets?

Snapchat planets are the eight positions in the Friend Solar System, according to Snapchat's Help Center. When a Snapchat+ subscriber opens a friend's Friendship Profile, a gold-ringed Best Friends badge appears if they are on that friend's Best Friends list. Tapping the badge shows which planet they occupy in that friend's Solar System.

Snapchat defines Best Friends as the eight friends a person Snaps and Chats with the most. So the Solar System only ever has eight planets.

Snapchat Planets List and Order: What Each Planet Means

The Snapchat planets order follows the real solar system, outward from the Sun. Snapchat's Help Center lists them from Mercury to Neptune.

Venus2ndTheir second most frequent contact

If you see no planet, you are not currently one of that friend's eight Best Friends. Best Friends lists update regularly, so your planet can move as messaging habits change.

Some fan sites give the planets labels such as "casual friend" or "acquaintance". Snapchat does not use those labels. Positions reflect how much you message each other, not how close the friendship is offline.

What Does "Snap BSF List Planets" Mean?

"BSF" is common slang for "best friend". People who search "snap BSF list planets" or "Snapchat BSF planets" are asking about the same thing: the planets that show your place on a friend's Best Friends list. The list and the planets are linked. A friend's Solar System is a visual version of their Best Friends list, with the friend at the centre.

Snapchat+ also offers an Extended Best Friends List. According to Snapchat's Help Center, it shows your current eight Best Friends, your next eight upcoming Best Friends and any movement since you last checked. It is turned on or off from the same Snapchat+ membership card.

How to Turn On the Snapchat Solar System

Snapchat says the Solar System is off by default for first-time Snapchat+ subscribers. To switch it on:

  1. Open Snapchat and tap your profile icon.
  2. Tap your Snapchat+ membership card to open the feature settings.
  3. Find Solar System and turn the toggle on.
  4. Open a friend's Friendship Profile and tap the Best Friends badge to see your planet.

You can turn it off at any time with the same toggle. Menu labels can differ slightly between app versions and between Android and iPhone.

Can You See Snapchat Planets Without Snapchat+?

No. Snapchat's Help Center describes the Friend Solar System as a Snapchat+ feature. Be cautious of websites, apps or "planet checker" tools that promise to show your planet for free. Snap's official app is the only way to see it, and you should never enter your Snapchat login on a third-party site.

Can Other People See Your Snapchat Planet?

No. In an April 5, 2024 post, Snap described the Solar System as "an optional, private feature that is only available with a Snapchat+ subscription." The company said it "does not show any other friends, it is not a numerical ranking, and it is not visible to anyone else."

Your full Best Friends list is also private. Snapchat says friends can tell whether they are on your list, but no one else can see the whole list.

Is Snapchatplanetsorders.com Official?

No. Snapchatplanetsorders.com is an independent fan website, not a Snap Inc. property. Its own disclaimer says it "has no official ties with Snap Inc." The official .com registry record from Verisign shows the domain was registered on October 16, 2024.

When we checked it on November 8, 2026, the site described the planets as your top eight friends orbiting you, with "you being the Sun." That differs from Snapchat's Help Center. There, the planet you see shows your position in a friend's Solar System, with that friend at the centre. The site also adds descriptions of each planet's colours and emoji that Snapchat's Help Center does not set out in text. For accurate details, check Snapchat's Help Center first.

Why Snapchat Turned the Solar System Off by Default

In its April 2024 post on Best Friends, Streaks and the Solar System, Snap announced it would turn the feature off by default. In the same post, Snap said some users felt pressure to reply to keep Snap Streaks going, and that it had made lost Streaks easier to restore. The Solar System remains available to Snapchat+ subscribers who choose to turn it on.

What Snapchat Planets Do Not Tell You

  • They measure activity, not affection. Positions are based on how often you Snap and Chat. A friend you see every day in person may rank lower than someone you message often.
  • They are one-sided. Your planet shows your place in a friend's list. Their position in your list can be different.
  • They change. Because Best Friends lists update regularly, a planet can move up or down within days.

Snapchat Planets FAQs

What is the Snapchat planets order?

Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune. Mercury means you are first on a friend's Best Friends list, and Neptune means you are eighth.

What does it mean if I am Mercury on Snapchat?

You are the person that friend Snaps and Chats with most, so you are first on their Best Friends list.

Why can't I see a planet on my friend's profile?

Either you are not one of their eight Best Friends, you do not have Snapchat+, or the Solar System feature is switched off in your Snapchat+ settings.

How often do Snapchat planets change?

Snapchat says Best Friends lists update regularly, so planets can change whenever messaging patterns change.

Is there a list of Snapchat planets on a website like snapchatplanetsorders.com?

Fan sites publish lists, but they are not official and can differ from Snapchat's own explanation. The order in this article matches Snapchat's Help Center.
