Domikyo.com Review: Old Posts, a Recycled Omicron Story and Guest Post Banners

What is domikyo.com? We checked its 2020 domain, 142 mostly 2021-22 posts, a recycled Omicron story, casino content and shared network contact email.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 9, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

Domikyo.com calls itself a site for "News and Articles Across Today's Key Issues", but most of its 142 posts were published in 2021 and 2022. Its recent articles include a recycled 2021 Omicron story and a post promoting a Bitcoin casino game. Its contact email and guest post marketplace banners match a network of blogs we have reviewed before. We checked the site on November 9, 2026.

Domikyo.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a WordPress blog with 142 posts on business, health, tech, finance and travel.
  • Domain: registered December 5, 2020.
  • Activity: 120 posts in 2021 and 2022, none in 2024 and 16 so far in 2026.
  • Network links: same Gmail contact as SparkPressFusion.com and MagazinePanda.com, plus Adoovy guest post marketplace banners.
  • Verdict: a guest post and advertising blog, not a news source.

What Is Domikyo.com?

The site describes itself as covering "the latest news, developments and researched stories across technology, finance, business, education, health, lifestyle, real estate and more." It has a full set of policy pages, including an Editorial Policy, DMCA and Disclaimer.

Its history tells a different story. A March 2021 Wayback Machine snapshot shows it as "Domikyo - Great Palace to share your Views", an open blog with posts by several contributors on topics such as impotence treatment and Botox.

Domain and Publishing History

  • Registration: the official Verisign registry record shows domikyo.com was registered on December 5, 2020, through Wild West Domains, and runs to 2032. You can check this with the ICANN registration lookup.
  • Post dates: 1 post dated 2020, 43 in 2021, 77 in 2022, 2 in 2023, none in 2024, 3 in 2025 and 16 in 2026.
  • Backdating: the oldest post is dated June 2020, before the domain was registered.

What Does Domikyo.com Publish Now?

  • "India aims to avoid 'pandemic roulette' : Omicron" (dated November 4, 2026). This is a 2021-era Omicron story with garbled wording such as "the unfold of the letter variant", and its "modified" date is earlier than its "published" date.
  • "Roobet's Mission Uncrossable advantage over other games" (June 30, 2026). It promotes a Bitcoin casino game with a followed link and no sponsored label or responsible-gambling notice.
  • "Mysterium VPN: What Should a No-Log VPN Actually Keep?" (August 25, 2026), with followed links to the VPN provider.
  • Health posts such as "7 Tips for Using Hearing Aids for the First Time" and "The Pros and Cons of Popular Anti-Aging Treatments", with no named medical reviewer.

Republishing an old news story with a new date misleads readers about how current it is. Google asks sites to label paid links, and the commercial links we checked were not labelled.

Does Domikyo.com Sell Advertising?

Yes. Its Advertise page says "sponsored content may be accepted following editorial review and will be identified where appropriate", and that pricing "may vary. Contact us for current options and applicable rates." No prices are published.

Every page also shows a "Guest Post Marketplace 2026" banner linking to Adoovy, the same marketplace advertised on TheModelEats.com and Yonosamachar.com.

Who Runs Domikyo.com?

Recent posts are credited to "Kevin". The site's WordPress data lists two accounts, "Kevin" and "suman", neither with a biography. The contact email is the same Gmail address listed on SparkPressFusion.com and MagazinePanda.com. That suggests a shared operator or network, although we could not confirm ownership.

Domikyo.com FAQs

What is domikyo.com?

A WordPress blog registered in 2020 that publishes general articles and accepts sponsored content.

Is Domikyo.com a news site?

Not in a meaningful sense. Most posts are old, and at least one recent "news" post is a recycled 2021 story.

Who owns Domikyo.com?

The site does not name an owner. Its contact email is shared with other blogs we have reviewed.

Checked on November 9, 2026, using the Verisign registry record, the Wayback Machine and the site's own pages and WordPress data. We will update this review if Domikyo.com responds or changes.
