Tozymagazine.com USA Review: From Birthday Wishes to Paid-Style Business Posts

Is tozymagazine.com a US magazine? No. We checked its 2024 domain, 356 posts, "usa" page, bylines and unlabelled business links. Here is what it is.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 9, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

Tozymagazine.com, often searched as "tozymagazine.com usa", is not a US magazine. It began in 2025 as a site for birthday and anniversary wishes and now mostly publishes commercial articles about electronics suppliers, law firms, home services and even ammunition retailers. We checked its domain record, archive history, authors and links on November 9, 2026.

Tozymagazine.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a WordPress blog with 356 posts, titled "Tozy Magazine - Crafting Sentiments for Every Occasion".
  • Domain: registered December 26, 2024. The registration runs out on December 26, 2026 unless it is renewed.
  • Content shift: wishes and quotes in 2025, then mainly B2B, legal and product posts by late 2026.
  • "USA" page: a page created in September 2026 calls itself "the official online magazine platform" for US readers.
  • Verdict: a content and link site, not a news publication.

What Is Tozymagazine.com?

The site's title and menu, with Marriage, Birthday, Anniversary and Thank You sections, describe a greeting-message site. Its About page calls it "a treasure trove of quotes, wishes, and messages". In 2025 that matched the content. A June 2025 Wayback Machine snapshot shows headlines such as "130 Best Heart Touching Birthday Quotes for Mom".

Even then, commercial posts appeared alongside the wishes, such as a solar installer in Yuba City and a guide to vehicle auctions.

Is There a "Tozymagazine.com USA" Edition?

No separate US edition exists. On September 8, 2026, the site published a roughly 2,500-word page at /tozymagazine-com-usa/ titled "Tozymagazine.com usa Digital Publication for Lifestyle". It describes the site as "the official online magazine platform" for readers across the United States and lists "advertising opportunities" and "guest contribution inquiries" as reasons to get in touch.

Pages built around a search phrase like "[site name] usa" exist mainly to capture people searching that phrase. Lookalike sites, such as tozymagazine.it.com, publish similar pages. Our earlier reviews of SparkPressFusion.com and MiloMagazine.co.uk found the same tactic.

Domain History

  • Registration: the official Verisign registry record shows tozymagazine.com was registered on December 26, 2024, through NameCheap. It is due to expire on December 26, 2026. You can check this with the ICANN registration lookup.
  • Backdated post: the oldest post is dated December 11, 2024, two weeks before the domain existed. WordPress dates can be edited.

Who Writes for Tozymagazine.com?

The site's WordPress data lists four bylines: "Devwiz" (271 posts, mostly the early wishes), "Muhammad Ijaz" (60), "Danny Welch" (23, all since October 23, 2026) and "Alex Alexander", whose account name is "active-linkers". None has a biography or listed expertise.

What Does It Publish Now?

Recent headlines have little to do with greetings:

  • "Sourcing the STM32F405RGT6: Silicon Specifications and How to Audit an MCU Supplier" (November 7, 2026)
  • "What Is the Process of Trying an Eminent Domain Case in Texas, and Who Do Attorneys Work With?" (November 3, 2026)
  • "How SANVO Optimizes Automated Mixing Equipment for Consistent Color Matching in Sealants" (November 4, 2026)
  • "Responsible Ownership FAQs: Storage, Transport, and Local Laws" (October 29, 2026), which links to an ammunition retailer
  • Crypto posts such as a review of a trading platform and a Bitcoin ATM guide

Links in these posts point to the businesses they describe, including an electronics supplier and a Texas law firm, with no sponsored or nofollow label. Google asks sites to label paid links.

Tozymagazine.com FAQs

What is tozymagazine.com usa?

A search phrase, and a page on tozymagazine.com targeting it. There is no separate US magazine.

Who owns Tozymagazine.com?

The site does not say, and the domain registration is private.

Does Tozymagazine.com accept guest posts?

It has no Write for Us page, but its USA page invites guest contribution and advertising enquiries.
