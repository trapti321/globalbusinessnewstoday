MyGreenBucks Net Jones: Who Is Kenneth Jones and Is MyGreenBucks.net Real?

Who is Kenneth Jones of MyGreenBucks.net? We checked the byline, the site's conflicting claims, its 1,497 posts and domain history. Here is what is true.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 8, 2026 • 7 min read
Share

People searching "mygreenbucks net jones" or "Kenneth MyGreenBucks .net" usually want to know who Kenneth Jones is and whether MyGreenBucks.net is a real finance platform. Here is what we found. MyGreenBucks.net is a WordPress content site, not an investment or rewards platform, and "Kenneth Jones" is a byline on that site with no verifiable real-world identity. We checked the domain record, its archive history and the site's own pages on November 8, 2026. For how we review sites like this, see our SparkPressFusion.com review.

MyGreenBucks.net and Kenneth Jones, at a glance:

  • What it is: a blog with about 1,497 posts on finance, crypto, business, casinos, betting and loans.
  • Who Kenneth Jones is: the display name on one of six author accounts on the site. The site's own articles about him contradict each other.
  • Domain: the name was used as early as 2009. The current registration dates from August 19, 2021.
  • Money: we found no sign-up, investment, rewards or payout feature. Do not send money to anyone claiming to represent it.
  • Verdict: an SEO content site. Its finance, crypto and gambling articles should not guide money decisions.

Who Is Kenneth Jones at MyGreenBucks.net?

On the site, "Kenneth Jones" is the name shown on posts from the author account whose web address ends in /author/james/. That account's posts range from finance tips to gambling and adult content. Archived copies show the "By Kenneth Jones" byline in use by November 2022. In September 2021, the same site credited posts to "James Gussie".

MyGreenBucks.net has also published several articles about Kenneth Jones, mostly under other house bylines such as "Xanthira Marzavina". Their claims conflict:

  • A December 2024 post is titled "Mygreenbucks Kenneth Jones: How Kenneth Jones Built a $2.5B Fintech Empire".
  • A post on the same day is titled "Kenneth MyGreenBucks .net Review: Shocking Truth About This Investment Platform Exposed". It presents the site as a possible scam.
  • An August 2026 "profile" says he joined MyGreenBucks in 2019. The current domain registration began in August 2021. The same profile opens by describing a different author, Xanthira Marzavina.

We found no independent record, professional profile or news coverage that confirms a real person behind the name, or any of the credentials attributed to him. A TycoonStory review also flagged claims linked to the name, including Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan experience, "500,000+ users" and Forbes 30 Under 30, and marked them all unverified. The safest reading is that Kenneth Jones is a byline, not a verified finance expert.

What Is MyGreenBucks.net?

The homepage describes the site as a place to "Master your money with free finance tips, budgeting guides, and crypto insights". Its menu lists Finance For Life, Managing Money, Cryptocurrency, Business Time, About Us and Contact. The About page, published in August 2022, describes a site "dedicated to helping you make and manage your money" but names no owner, company or founder. The only contact is a form and a generic email address.

Some websites describe MyGreenBucks.net as a rewards app, gift-card earning platform or "eco finance" service. We found no such feature: no sign-up flow, wallet, payouts or investment product in the site's navigation.

What Does MyGreenBucks.net Publish?

The site's sitemaps list about 1,497 posts, from September 2022 to November 6, 2026. In the post web addresses, "casino" appears 102 times, "slot" 79, "crypto" 70, "betting" 35 and "loan" 33. Examples include:

  • "Roobet Referral Code 2026: Rewards, Bonuses and How It Works"
  • Posts about betting sites outside GamStop, the UK's gambling self-exclusion scheme
  • Nonsense-titled posts such as "Bucksmygrene Neutrino Waffle IPO: What Investors Need To Know In 2026"
  • Many posts that target misspellings of the site's own name

External links in the posts we checked, including links to a lender, a tax-relief firm and a betting-site list, carried no sponsored or nofollow label. Google asks sites to label paid links that way.

Domain History

  • 2009: the Wayback Machine shows mygreenbucks.net as a blog titled "Mygreenbucks.net| Autopilotincome101.com|Blogging 5.0", with movie and celebrity posts.
  • 2021: the official Verisign registry record shows the current registration began on August 19, 2021, through GoDaddy. The first snapshot after that shows personal finance posts.
  • 2022 onwards: the "Kenneth Jones" byline appears, and publishing grows to well over a thousand posts.

Does MyGreenBucks.net Sell Guest Posts?

The site has no Write for Us page. However, a PeoplePerHour listing offered a "Finance Guest Post Backlink on mygreenbucks.net" for $100, with a "dofollow" link. The listing is now marked inactive and had a buyer review dated May 2025. Its seller is a third party, and we could not confirm a link to the site's owner.

Is MyGreenBucks.net Legit or a Scam?

We found no regulator warning or credible scam report. The site does not appear to take money from readers. It is not a legitimate source of financial guidance either:

  • No named owner or editor, and no verifiable author credentials.
  • Conflicting, unverifiable claims about its "founder".
  • Large volumes of casino, betting and loan content, with no labels on commercial links.

If anyone contacts you claiming to be Kenneth Jones from MyGreenBucks and offers investment returns or payouts, treat it as a red flag. You can check investment firms through the FINRA BrokerCheck tool in the US or the FCA register in the UK.

MyGreenBucks Net Jones FAQs

Who is Kenneth Jones from MyGreenBucks.net?

A byline on MyGreenBucks.net. The site publishes conflicting claims about him, and we found no independent evidence of his identity or credentials.

Is MyGreenBucks.net an investment platform?

No. It is a blog. We found no investment, rewards or payout features.

Is MyGreenBucks.net safe?

Reading it is low-risk, but do not act on its finance, crypto, gambling or loan content without independent advice, and never send money to anyone using its name.

When was MyGreenBucks.net created?

The name was in use by 2009. The current registration dates from August 19, 2021.

Information was checked on November 8, 2026, using the Verisign registry record, the Wayback Machine, the site's own pages and sitemaps, and the third-party listings linked above. We will update this review if MyGreenBucks.net responds or provides verifiable details.
