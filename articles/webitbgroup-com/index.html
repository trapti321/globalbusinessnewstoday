Webitbgroup.com: Why the Site Is Offline and What Web ITB Group Was

Webitbgroup.com is offline as of Nov 8, 2026. We traced it from a 2011 Indian SEO agency to a guest-post site and checked what its DNS failure means.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 8, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

Webitbgroup.com is offline. As of November 8, 2026, the domain does not resolve: its nameservers refuse to answer, so browsers cannot find the site. Until 2025 it ran a guest-blogging site called "Web ITB Group". Before that, from 2011, it was an Indian web design and SEO agency. Here is what we could verify about webitbgroup.com and what its outage means. It is one of several small guest post sites we have checked, alongside JournalWord.com and CNLawBlog.

Webitbgroup.com at a glance:

  • Status: unreachable. DNS lookups fail as of November 8, 2026.
  • Domain: registered January 16, 2011, and renewed to January 2033.
  • Last seen online: the Wayback Machine's last working capture is August 15, 2025.
  • What it was: a "News Articles And Guest Blogging" site taking guest posts on mixed topics.
  • Verdict: do not pay for a guest post on it, and do not rely on links pointing to it.

Is Webitbgroup.com Down?

Yes. On November 8, 2026, requests to webitbgroup.com and www.webitbgroup.com failed with a DNS error. A lookup through Google Public DNS returned SERVFAIL, because the domain's nameservers refused to answer for it. The domain is still registered, but the site cannot be reached until its DNS is fixed.

We cannot say why the DNS broke. Common reasons include an expired hosting account, nameservers being changed or the site being abandoned.

Domain History: From SEO Agency to Guest Post Site

  • Registration: the official Verisign registry record, viewable through the ICANN registration lookup, shows webitbgroup.com was registered on January 16, 2011, through Wild West Domains, and renewed to January 16, 2033.
  • 2011: the earliest Wayback Machine capture shows an Indian agency site titled "Seo services, web designing, web development, web hosting, website promotion", with packages and careers pages.
  • 2025: the last working capture, from August 15, 2025, shows "Web ITB Group | News Articles And Guest Blogging - Best Blog post site, Article, News, Write For US". It ran on WordPress with a plugin named "seo-automated-link-building".

What Did Webitbgroup.com Publish?

Archived posts, bylined "webitbgroup", were dated from 2018 to 2025 and covered unrelated topics. Examples include:

  • "The Most Magical Castles to Visit in the Loire Valley" (May 1, 2025)
  • "Understanding the Stages of Grief After a Loss" (June 4, 2025)
  • "How to Find the Right Lawyer for Your Academic Dismissal Appeal" (April 12, 2023)
  • "How to Resolve and Fix QuickBooKs Error 6175"

Outbound links in archived posts, such as links to a law firm and a funeral home, carried no nofollow or sponsored label.

Did Webitbgroup.com Accept Guest Posts?

Yes. Its archived Write for Us page said the site was "specially made for guest bloggers". It asked for at least 800 words, an author bio and no more than three outbound links. It banned adult and casino content and affiliate links. No price was published.

What the Outage Means

  • If you bought a post there: your article and its link are no longer reachable. Search engines will eventually drop links from pages that stay unreachable.
  • If someone offers you a post on it now: do not pay. The site is offline.
  • If you are researching the name: some search results may still show cached pages from before the outage.

This is a wider risk of buying links on small guest-post sites: they can disappear without notice. For other sites we have checked, see our reviews of JournalWord.com and UploadBlog.com.

Webitbgroup.com FAQs

Is webitbgroup.com working?

No. As of November 8, 2026, the domain's DNS fails and the site cannot be loaded.

What was webitbgroup.com?

Originally an Indian web design and SEO agency site (2011), and later a guest-blogging site called Web ITB Group.

Is webitbgroup.com still registered?

Yes. According to the Verisign registry, the registration runs to January 16, 2033.

Information was checked on November 8, 2026, using the Verisign registry record, DNS lookups and Wayback Machine captures. We will update this article if webitbgroup.com comes back online.
