Detroit Pistons vs Orlando Magic Match Player Stats: Game 7 Box Score and Full Series

Pistons vs Magic match player stats from Detroit's 116-94 Game 7 win on May 3, 2026: Banchero 38, Cunningham 32 and 12 assists, Harris 30, all 7 results.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 8, 2026 • 6 min read
Share

The Detroit Pistons beat the Orlando Magic 116-94 in Game 7 of their first-round playoff series on Sunday, May 3, 2026, at Little Caesars Arena. Detroit came back from 3-1 down to win the series 4-3. It was the most recent Pistons vs Magic game. Below are the full match player stats from the ESPN box score.

Pistons vs Magic at a glance: Pistons 116, Magic 94 · Game 7, East first round · May 3, 2026 · Little Caesars Arena, Detroit · Attendance 20,062. Top scorers: Paolo Banchero 38 (ORL), Cade Cunningham 32 and 12 assists (DET), Tobias Harris 30 (DET).

Top-seeded Detroit (60-22) became the 15th team in NBA history to come back from a 3-1 series deficit, and won its first playoff series since 2008. Cunningham and Harris were the first Pistons pair to both score 30 points in a playoff game since 1977, according to the Associated Press.

What the Stats Show

Detroit won the game in the second quarter, scoring 40 points to turn a two-point deficit into a 60-49 halftime lead. The Pistons then held Orlando to 15 points in the third. Detroit's 30 assists to Orlando's 18 show how much more freely its offence moved.

"We were pushed to the limit," Cunningham said, according to the AP. "And it got us back to playing the basketball that we knew we were capable of." Pistons coach J.B. Bickerstaff said, "This is a special group. And you can't count us out." Magic coach Jamahl Mosley said, "We just couldn't find the basket."

Cunningham averaged 32.4 points across the seven-game series, according to the AP. Detroit then advanced to face the winner of the Cleveland Cavaliers' series with Toronto; for Cleveland's regular-season numbers against Boston, see our Celtics vs Cavaliers player stats.
