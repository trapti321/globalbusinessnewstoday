JournalWord.com Review: Is Journalword Com a Journaling App or an SEO Blog?

Is journalword com a journaling tool? No. We checked its 2010-2023 domain history, bylines, 115 posts and its bulk link pages. Here is what it really is.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 8, 2026 • 6 min read
Share

JournalWord.com (often searched as "journalword com") is a WordPress blog whose posts are largely reviews and guides about other websites, plus general business, health and crypto articles. Some web pages describe it as a journaling app, but it is not one. We checked the site, its domain history, authors, link pages and recent posts on November 8, 2026. It follows a pattern we found in our NewsGiga.com review: a general blog that targets searches for other websites' names.

JournalWord.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a multi-topic WordPress blog, not a journaling or writing tool.
  • Domain: the name was used from 2010, and the current registration dates from March 5, 2023.
  • Posts: 115, the newest dated November 5, 2026. Most are by one byline, "Devin Haney".
  • Links: it hosts pages listing hundreds of outbound links to other blogs.
  • Verdict: an SEO-driven blog. Do not rely on it for health, crypto or financial decisions.

What Is JournalWord.com?

The site's title is "Journalword | journalword.com - Where Content Comes Alive". It describes itself as "your go-to platform for insightful articles, fresh ideas, and useful knowledge across topics like technology, business, lifestyle, and more." It runs on WordPress and has only three pages: About Us, Contact Us, and Terms and Conditions.

Some other websites describe JournalWord.com as a digital journaling or productivity tool that turns messy notes into order. The live site has no such tool. It is a blog.

Domain History: 2010 Splash Page, 2013 Indonesian Blog, 2023 Re-Registration

  • 2010 to 2016: the Wayback Machine shows a "Click here to enter" splash page in 2010. In 2013 the domain hosted an Indonesian-language business blog, "Journal Word – Inspirasi Untuk Usaha". By 2016 it showed a "Click here to proceed" page.
  • 2016 to 2023: there are no archive captures.
  • 2023: the official Verisign registry record, which you can check with the ICANN registration lookup, shows the current registration began on March 5, 2023, through NameCheap. The first post on the current site is dated March 30, 2023.

A new registration date on a domain with earlier history usually means the name lapsed and was registered again by a new owner. We found nothing linking the 2013 blog to the current site.

Who Writes for JournalWord.com?

The site's author sitemap lists four bylines. "Devin Haney" is credited with 87 of the 115 posts, "Shivi Hyde" with 11, and two others with one each. None has a biography. The contact page gives only a Gmail address and says it was made with a "Contact Us Page Generator". The site names no owner or editor.

What Does JournalWord.com Publish?

Many posts are about other websites and brands, such as SoSoActive, TheLifestyleEdge.com, Blookle and Yonopress.com. Others cover health, crypto exchanges, a prescription erectile dysfunction drug and a poker app. Recent headlines include:

  • "BMI vs Waist-to-Height Ratio: Which Says More About Health?" (November 5, 2026)
  • "SoSoActive: Making Business, Finance, and Technology Easier to Understand" (September 9, 2026)
  • "SurgeU Reviews: What Students Say About the Program After Enrolling" (August 18, 2026)
  • "Wellhealth how to build muscle tag – journalword.com" (October 21, 2023)

Publishing has slowed: there was a burst of 26 posts in January 2024, but only five in 2026.

JournalWord.com's Link Pages

Two pages stand out. A page at /backlinks/ is little more than about 317 links, each with the anchor text "Link", pointing to other blogs and none labelled nofollow. A page at /websites/ lists 470 more outbound links. Several sites that JournalWord.com writes "reviews" about also appear in its /backlinks/ list.

Pages that exist mainly to pass links between sites are what Google's spam policies describe as link schemes. Google decides how to treat any particular site.

Does JournalWord.com Accept Guest Posts?

There is no Write for Us, guest post or advertising page; those addresses return "page not found". The contact page invites emails about "advertising, or anything else". We found no published prices.

JournalWord.com FAQs

What is journalword com?

A WordPress blog, re-registered in 2023, that publishes reviews of other websites alongside general business, health and crypto articles.

Is JournalWord.com a journaling app?

No. Despite descriptions on some other sites, it has no journaling or note-taking features.

Who runs JournalWord.com?

The site does not name an owner. Most posts are credited to "Devin Haney", who has no biography.

Is it safe to use JournalWord.com?

The site is a standard blog and did not ask us for personal information. Treat its health, crypto and financial content as unverified.

Information was checked on November 8, 2026, using the Verisign registry record, the Wayback Machine and the site's own pages and WordPress data. We will update this review if JournalWord.com responds or changes.
