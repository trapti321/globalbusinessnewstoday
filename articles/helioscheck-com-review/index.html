Helioscheck.com Review: A $40 Guest Post Blog, Not a Solar Checker

Helioscheck.com is a 42-post WordPress blog, not a solar tool. It sells $40 guest posts and shares a Gmail contact with a known blog network.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 11, 2026 • 4 min read
Share

Helioscheck.com sounds like a solar or sun-checking tool, but it is a small WordPress blog called "Helios Check" with 42 general-interest posts and no tool, app or service. We checked the site on November 11, 2026. Its posts are sold as paid guest post placements through the Adoovy marketplace for $40, its only contact is the same Gmail account used by a network of guest-post blogs we have reviewed, and some posts are dated months before the domain was registered.

Helioscheck.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a WordPress blog with 42 posts, six in each of seven categories, all credited to "Adoosylinks".
  • Is it a solar or checking tool? No. There is no calculator, checker, account or download.
  • Domain: registered May 11, 2024, through Wild West Domains, yet its oldest post is dated December 4, 2023.
  • Network link: same Gmail contact as SnapJotz, QuikConsole and SparkPressFusion.
  • Verdict: a paid-placement content site, not an independent publication.

What Is Helioscheck.com?

Helioscheck.com uses the homepage title "Helios Check" and runs on WordPress with the Newsever theme from AF Themes, behind Cloudflare. On November 11, 2026, its public WordPress data listed 42 posts. Business, Education, Finance, Home Improvement, Law, Lifestyle and Technology each had exactly six posts.

Typical titles include "Unlocking Your Brand's True North: Where Our Brand Positioning Focuses On", "Beyond the Blueprint: Rethinking Vanguard Asset Allocation for Real Life" and "When Safety Falls Short: The Critical Role of a Negligent Security Lawyer". The newest post is dated March 14, 2025, so the site has not published anything new in about 20 months.

The site has no WordPress pages at all. Its About, Contact, Privacy Policy and Write for Us addresses all returned "404 Not Found" when we checked.

Who Runs Helioscheck.com?

No person or company is named. The only WordPress user is "Adoosylinks", with no biography. The same byline appears on TheModelEats.com and FundFireInsight.com, and it closely resembles Adoovy, the guest post marketplace whose banner sits in Helioscheck's sidebar.

The footer's email link is hidden by Cloudflare's email protection. Decoded, it is the same Gmail account listed on SnapJotz.com, QuikConsole.com, Adstotally.com, Domikyo.com and SparkPressFusion.com. A shared address suggests common management, but we could not confirm who owns these sites.

Paid Guest Posts on Helioscheck.com

Adoovy's listing for helioscheck.com, checked on November 11, 2026, sells an "advertorial" on the site with these terms:

Adoovy listing itemWhat it says
Price$40.00
Links"Permanent and do-follow"
Turnaround24 to 48 hours
Authority scoresDA 46, DR 26
Traffic0 Ahrefs traffic, 0 Semrush traffic
Other claim"Google News Indexed"

The listing's own figures show zero estimated search traffic, so a placement would reach few readers. Google's spam policies also treat buying or selling links that pass ranking credit as link spam. When we scanned all 42 posts, none contained a link to another website.

Helioscheck.com Domain History

  • March 2024: Wayback Machine copies of helioscheck.com subdomains show a domain parking page, before the current registration.
  • May 11, 2024: domain registered through Wild West Domains, according to the Verisign .com registry. It expires May 11, 2029.
  • May 2024: an archived copy shows a parking redirect page.
  • January to March 2025: a different "Helios Check" blog with posts by "rockypipes", such as "Planet Fitness Open on Christmas Day" and "Blink Fitness Annual Fee: What You Need to Know", under General, Health, Tech and Travel categories. Those posts now return 404 errors.
  • December 10, 2025: 37 of the current 42 posts show this as their last-modified date, which points to a bulk rebuild of the site.
  • January 2026 onward: archived copies show the current Adoosylinks posts.

The current posts carry dates from December 2023 to March 2025, all stamped at exactly midnight. The seven posts dated December 2023 predate the May 2024 registration, so those dates were set by hand or imported.

What the Top Search Result Misses

The buzzsport.co.uk guide that ranks first for "helioscheck.com", dated October 12, 2026, correctly identifies the Newsever theme, the Adoosylinks byline and the Adoovy listing. It does not mention the May 2024 registration date, the earlier version of the site, the $40 price, Adoovy's zero-traffic figures or the shared Gmail contact.

Is Helioscheck.com Safe?

We found no casino, CBD or payday loan content, no downloads and no request for payment from readers. Reading it is low-risk. But it names no owner or editor, has no contact page, and exists to sell placements. Do not rely on its law or finance posts, and be wary of any "Helioscheck" solar checker or app offered elsewhere; the site has none.

Helioscheck.com FAQs

Is Helioscheck.com a solar or sun-check tool?

No. Despite the name, it is a general blog with no tools.

Who owns Helioscheck.com?

No owner is named. Its contact is a Gmail account shared with several other guest-post blogs.

Does Helioscheck.com accept guest posts?

Yes, through Adoovy, which lists a $40 advertorial with do-follow links.

When was Helioscheck.com created?

The current registration dates from May 11, 2024.

Checked on November 11, 2026, using the site's own pages and public WordPress data, the Verisign registry record, the Wayback Machine, the Adoovy listing and the articles ranking for this search. We will update this review if Helioscheck.com responds.
