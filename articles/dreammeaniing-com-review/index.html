Dreammeaniing.com Review: The Dream Meaning Site Behind the Double-I Spelling

What is dreammeaniing com? We checked the 2025 dream site, how it differs from dreammeaning.com, its authors and its shift to unrelated commercial posts.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 8, 2026 • 6 min read
Share

Dreammeaniing.com (spelled with a double "i", and often searched as "dreammeaniing com") is a young WordPress site that started as a dream-interpretation blog. Since late 2026 it has published more and more unrelated commercial posts. It is not the long-running dreammeaning.com, which has a different owner history and currently does not load. We checked both domains on November 8, 2026, along with dreammeaniing.com's own pages. The switch from a single topic to paid-looking posts mirrors what we found in our TheModelEats.com review.

Dreammeaniing.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a dream-meaning blog with 276 posts. About 218 are dream interpretations.
  • Domain: registered November 24, 2025. Its first post is dated the same day.
  • Recent shift: since around September 2026, many new posts cover floor cleaners, vapes, peptides and car scrappage.
  • Contact: an email address on a different company's domain.
  • Verdict: fine for light reading about dreams. It is not a source for health or mental-health advice.

What Is Dreammeaniing.com?

The site's title is "Dream meaniing - dreammeaniing.com". Its About page says it "exists for one purpose: to explain dreams in clear, simple language. No mystical nonsense." Its pages include an "Unlock Your Dream Meaning" AI chat widget and Google AdSense ads. The Disclaimer page says the content is "for educational and entertainment purposes only."

The menu has Dream Meanings, About, Contact, Disclaimer and Privacy Policy pages. There is no Write for Us or advertising page.

Dreammeaniing.com vs Dreammeaning.com

The misspelled name matters. According to the Verisign .com registry, the correctly spelled dreammeaning.com was registered in December 2003. In 2008 the Wayback Machine shows it as "The Leading Hypnotherapy Site on the Net", and in our test on November 8, 2026 it did not load.

Dreammeaniing.com was registered on November 24, 2025, through Hostinger, according to the registry record (see the ICANN registration lookup), uses different nameservers and has no archive history before April 2026. We found no ownership link between the two. A third lookalike, dreameaninng.com, was registered in November 2025 and is linked from dreammeaniing.com's comment section.

Registering a near-identical misspelling of an established domain name is a common way to capture search traffic. Readers looking for the original site should check the spelling carefully.

Who Writes for Dreammeaniing.com?

The site lists two author accounts. One, named "Admin", has a bio describing "Henry" as "a passionate dream analyst and writer". The other, "Armani Sharp", has no bio. No qualifications in psychology or sleep science are listed. The only contact address is on trustautomations.com, a separate domain, and the site names no owner.

What Does Dreammeaniing.com Publish?

The site's 276 posts run from November 24, 2025, to November 2, 2026. Most of the early posts are dream interpretations, for example "Fever Dream Meaning: Guilt, Grief, or Mental Overload?" and "Dreaming About Being Arrested? What It Means, and What to Do If It Actually Happens".

From around September 2026, much of the new content has nothing to do with dreams:

  • "Best Hard Floor Cleaners for Black Friday Shoppers in Australia" (October 29, 2026)
  • "Tirzepatide Research Peptides: Understanding Purity, Storage, and Handling" (September 4, 2026)
  • "What Is the Best Vape Flavor and Device for You?" (September 9, 2026)
  • Posts on car scrappage, proprietary trading firms and hospital claims

A post on hospital claims links to a claims company without a sponsored label. Google asks sites to use that label for paid links. A dream site that starts publishing unrelated product and service articles is a typical sign that it is selling placements.

Can You Trust Dream Interpretations Online?

Dream interpretation is not an exact science, and the meaning of a dream is personal. The Sleep Foundation notes that researchers still debate why we dream. Treat any site's interpretations as entertainment. If dreams are distressing, frequent nightmares disturb your sleep, or a post about "guilt, grief, or mental overload" sounds familiar, speak to a doctor or licensed therapist.

Dreammeaniing.com FAQs

What is dreammeaniing com?

A dream-interpretation blog registered in November 2025, now also publishing unrelated commercial articles.

Is dreammeaniing.com the same as dreammeaning.com?

No. They are different domains with different registration histories, and we found no link between them.

Who runs dreammeaniing.com?

The site does not name an owner. Its contact email is on a separate company's domain.
