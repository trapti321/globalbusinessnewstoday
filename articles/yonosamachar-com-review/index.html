Yonosamachar.com Review: What Yono Samachar Is and Why It Isn't SBI YONO

Is yonosamachar com linked to SBI YONO or Yono games? No. We checked its 2023 domain, 86 posts, loan and betting content and missing policy pages.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 8, 2026 • 6 min read
Share

Yonosamachar.com (often searched as "yonosamachar com") is a small WordPress blog with mixed posts on loan apps, movie download sites, sports betting and general topics. Despite the name, it has no connection to SBI's YONO banking app or to "Yono" gaming apps, and it has not published anything new since August 2025. We checked the site, its domain, posts and policies on November 8, 2026. It shares several traits with SparkPressFusion.com, which we reviewed the same day.

Yonosamachar.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a multi-topic WordPress blog with 86 posts.
  • Domain: registered March 18, 2023, though some posts are dated as early as 2015.
  • Author: one byline, "Roland".
  • Pages: no About, Contact, Privacy Policy or Disclaimer page.
  • Last post: August 29, 2025.
  • Verdict: not a news source. Treat its loan and betting content with caution.

What Is Yonosamachar.com?

"Samachar" means "news" in Hindi, but Yono Samachar is not a news outlet. It is a WordPress blog with categories for Automotive, Business, Education, Entertainment, Fashion, Finance, Health, Lifestyle, Technology and Travel. The homepage has no description and the site has no WordPress pages at all: requests for About, Contact, Privacy Policy and Write for Us pages return "page not found".

The only contact is a Gmail address in the footer. It is the same address listed on SparkPressFusion.com, which suggests the two sites share an operator or manager.

Is Yonosamachar.com Related to SBI YONO or Yono Games?

No. We searched the site's posts and sitemap. "Yono" appears only as part of the site's name. There is no content about State Bank of India's YONO app, and no Yono rummy, teen patti or APK download content. Anyone looking for the SBI YONO app should use the official app stores or SBI's website, not third-party sites.

Domain History and Post Dates

  • Registration: the official Verisign registry record shows yonosamachar.com was registered on March 18, 2023, through Wild West Domains. You can confirm it with the ICANN registration lookup.
  • Backdated posts: the oldest posts are dated December 2015, such as "10 secrets about hot songs the government is hiding". That is more than seven years before the domain was registered, so these appear to be placeholder or demo posts with old dates.
  • Activity: real publishing ran mainly from 2023 to August 29, 2025. Nothing new has appeared in over 14 months.

What Does Yonosamachar.com Publish?

The site's 86 posts are all credited to "Roland", and the author's web address uses a different name. Topics include:

  • Loan apps: "RBI Approved Loan Apps: Your Ultimate List" and posts on credit (CIBIL) scores.
  • Movie download sites: "Netninja: A Movie Downloader for Android – Your Gateway to Free Entertainment" and FzTvSeries.
  • Sports betting: guides to betting platforms in Zambia, such as a Castlebet guide titled "Sports Betting in Zambia: A Comprehensive Guide to Online Football Betting with Castlebet".
  • Trading: "What's New in MT5? Top Features and Updates", which links to a forex broker without a sponsored label.

Only one betting post carries a responsible-gambling disclaimer. The site has no age warning or privacy policy, and its loan-app posts have no financial disclaimers. Sites that promote free movie downloads may also point readers to pirated content, which can carry legal and malware risks.

Does Yonosamachar.com Sell Guest Posts?

The site has no guest post page, but every page shows a "Guest Post Marketplace 2026" banner linking to Adoovy. That is the same marketplace advertised on TheModelEats.com. We found no published price for a post on Yonosamachar.com.

Yonosamachar.com FAQs

What is yonosamachar com?

A multi-topic WordPress blog registered in 2023. Despite its name, it is not a news site and is not connected to SBI YONO.

Is Yonosamachar.com still active?

The site is online, but its newest post is dated August 29, 2025.

Who runs Yonosamachar.com?

The site does not say. It lists only a Gmail contact address, which also appears on SparkPressFusion.com.

Information was checked on November 8, 2026, using the Verisign registry record, the Wayback Machine and the site's own pages and WordPress data. We will update this review if Yonosamachar.com responds or changes.
