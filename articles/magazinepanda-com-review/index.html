MagazinePanda.com Review: A Blog That Mostly Writes About Itself

What is magazinepanda.com? We checked its 2021 domain, 91 posts, self-review headlines, template team bios and shared contact email with other blogs.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 9, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

MagazinePanda.com is a WordPress blog that now describes itself as a site about "digital life, technology & culture". Most of its recent articles mention the site's own name in the headline or text, and its contact address is the same Gmail account we found on several other link-selling blogs. We checked its domain, archive history, team page and links on November 9, 2026.

MagazinePanda.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a 91-post WordPress blog, titled "Magazinepanda.com | Digital Life, Technology & Culture".
  • Domain: registered March 9, 2021.
  • Self-promotion: 88 of 91 posts match a search for "magazinepanda", and recent headlines include two "MagazinePanda.com Review" posts.
  • Team page: lists SEO job titles and bios that appear to be template text.
  • Verdict: an SEO content site, not an independent magazine.

What Is MagazinePanda.com?

The site's description says it "delivers clear insights on digital life, technology, AI, online culture, product reviews, and smarter choices." Its menu is mostly policy pages: About, News, Contact, Disclaimer, Terms, DMCA and Privacy. Its largest categories are Home Improvement, General, Technology and Travel, and many categories are empty.

A 2023 Wayback Machine snapshot shows a different pitch: "Magazine Panda - Explore the infinity Knowledge", described as "a leading News Portal", with generic finance and insurance tips.

Why So Many Posts Mention MagazinePanda.com

Recent headlines often name the site itself:

  • "Growing an Online Store: How magazinepanda.com Covers E-Commerce Strategies for New Entrepreneurs" (October 22, 2026)
  • "MagazinePanda.com Review: Features, Content, Categories & User Guide" (August 26, 2026)
  • "MagazinePanda.com Review (2026): Features, Benefits & Complete Content Guide" (August 3, 2026)
  • "AI Infrastructure Costs in 2026: What Founders Need to Know Before Scaling | magazinepanda.com" (September 11, 2026)

A site reviewing itself is not independent evidence of quality. Repeating the brand name targets people who search for it, the same pattern we found on CNLawBlog and Puzutask.com.

Other posts profile other websites or link to businesses, such as a live-streaming app, a remote-work site and an e-commerce tool. The links carry no sponsored label. Older posts include slot machine and casino content.

Who Runs MagazinePanda.com?

Recent posts are credited to an account called "Magazinepanda.com". The About page lists an "Editorial Team" with SEO job titles such as "On-Page Executive" and "Off-Page Executive". Some team bios appear to be leftover template text: one describes a person called "Lawrence" under a different name, and another describes a "forest-goer". We could not verify any team member.

The contact page's email address is the same Gmail account listed on SparkPressFusion.com, Yonosamachar.com and as the guest post address on CNLawBlog. That suggests a shared operator or network, though we could not confirm ownership.

Domain History

  • Registration: the official Verisign registry record shows magazinepanda.com was registered on March 9, 2021, through Wild West Domains, and runs to 2030. You can check this with the ICANN registration lookup.
  • Backdated content: one post is dated March 2019, two years before the domain was registered, and several pages carry 2019 and 2020 dates.

MagazinePanda.com FAQs

What is magazinepanda.com?

A WordPress blog registered in 2021 that publishes general technology, home and lifestyle articles, many of which mention the site's own name.

Is MagazinePanda.com a real magazine?

It is a website, not a print or established digital magazine, and its editorial team could not be verified.

Who owns MagazinePanda.com?

The site does not name an owner. Its contact email is shared with several other blogs we have reviewed.
