TheModelEats.com Review: Is It Legit? What the Site Is and Who Runs It

Is TheModelEats.com legit? We checked its domain history, single byline, guest post links and publishing activity. Here is what the site is today.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 3, 2026 •Updated Nov 8, 2026 • 7 min read
Share

TheModelEats.com has a name that suggests food or modelling, but the site now publishes articles on employment law, behavioral health, education and home improvement, all under one byline: "Adoosylinks." We checked its domain record, its archive history and its own sitemap on November 3, 2026 to find out what changed.

TheModelEats.com at a glance (checked November 8, 2026):

  • What it is: a general WordPress blog covering business, law, health, education and home improvement, despite the food-and-modelling name.
  • Who writes it: one byline, "Adoosylinks", with no biography.
  • Domain: archived since 2013, re-registered on May 19, 2024.
  • Activity: 41 posts, the newest dated June 29, 2025.
  • Verdict: not a reliable source for legal or health advice, and a weak choice for paid guest posts.

The short version: the domain has existed since at least 2013, appears to have lapsed, and was registered again in May 2024. Since then it has published 41 posts and carries a banner advertising a guest post marketplace. Here is what we verified and what that means if you are thinking of reading it or buying a post there.

What Is TheModelEats.com Today?

TheModelEats.com is a WordPress site built on a free news theme. Its menu lists Business, Education, Finance, Health, Home Improvement, Law, Lifestyle and Technology. The homepage has no About, Contact or Write for Us page, and the only contact option is an email link hidden behind Cloudflare's email protection.

Recent headlines include "Unlocking California's Ban: Why Your Non-Compete is Likely Null and Void Under Code 16600" and "Charting a Course Through Life's Disruptions: Understanding Exodus Behavioral Health." Many name a specific company, clinic or school, a pattern common on sites that sell sponsored posts.

Who Writes for TheModelEats.com?

Every post we saw is credited to "Adoosylinks," and the site's own user sitemap lists that as its only author. The author page has no biography. The name closely resembles Adoovy, the guest post marketplace advertised in the site's sidebar. The same byline appears on FundFireInsight.com.

For readers, that means no named writer stands behind articles on topics such as employment law and mental health, where expert input matters most.

Is TheModelEats.com Still Publishing?

Not much. The newest post on the homepage and author page is dated June 29, 2025, and the one before it April 21, 2025. The site's sitemap lists 41 posts in total. Several were last modified in January 2026, but we found nothing new published in the 16 months since June 2025.

The Domain's History: From 2013 to a 2024 Re-Registration

The Wayback Machine has snapshots of themodeleats.com going back to March 2013. The 2015, 2017 and 2019 snapshots we opened all show the same placeholder: "Our website is currently down for maintenance but we will be back with a beautiful new site soon," with an email address beginning "iza@."

The archive has no captures between May 2022 and May 2024. The current public registration record, which you can check with the ICANN lookup tool, shows a registration date of May 19, 2024 through Wild West Domains, running to 2029. A fresh registration date on a domain with years of earlier history usually means it expired and was registered again by a new owner.

A Marketing Lad review links the original domain to Izabella Rekiel, a model and content creator. We could not confirm that from public records, and nothing on the current site connects her to its content.

Why Expired Domains Get Reused for Guest Posts

An older domain keeps the backlinks it earned before it expired. Some buyers re-register such domains, add new content and sell posts on them, hoping the old links make the new pages rank faster.

Google's spam policies define "expired domain abuse" as a case where "an expired domain name is purchased and repurposed primarily to manipulate search rankings by hosting content that provides little to no value to users." Whether a particular site crosses that line is Google's call, but the pattern on TheModelEats.com is the one the policy describes.

Does TheModelEats.com Sell Guest Posts?

The site itself has no guest post page. Its sidebar shows a "Guest Post Marketplace 2026" banner that redirects to Adoovy, which describes itself as a "Digital PR & Blog Posting Service." That banner link carries no rel="sponsored" label, which Google asks sites to use for paid or advertising links.

Marketing Lad reported that TheModelEats.com was listed on Adoovy for $40 per post, with a permanent "do-follow" link and a 24 to 48 hour turnaround. Adoovy's homepage did not mention the site when we checked, so we could not verify the listing or the price. The same Adoovy banner appears on Yonosamachar.com.

How Much Traffic Does TheModelEats.com Get?

We have no first-hand traffic data. Marketing Lad said an Ahrefs check found zero estimated organic traffic and zero ranking keywords, with backlinks down 79% from their peak. Those are third-party tool estimates reported by a link-building agency, so treat them as indicative, not exact.

Marketing Lad and an earlier Ranktracker review also reported that the site returned a "403 Forbidden" error to their checks. In our test on November 3, 2026, the homepage loaded normally with a 200 response.

Should You Buy a Guest Post on TheModelEats.com?

We would not recommend it for a business that cares about its search reputation. Here is how the site measures up:

TheModelEats.com FAQs

Is TheModelEats.com legit?

It is a real, working website, but it does not show the signs of a trustworthy publication: there are no named writers, no About or Contact page, no topic focus and no new posts since June 2025. Treat its articles as unverified.

What is TheModelEats.com about?

Despite its name, it is not a food or modelling site. It publishes short articles on business, law, health, education, finance, home improvement, lifestyle and technology, many of which name a specific company or service.

Who owns TheModelEats.com?

The site does not name an owner. Public registration data shows the domain was re-registered on May 19, 2024, through Wild West Domains. The current site names no owner or publisher.

Does TheModelEats.com accept guest posts?

It has no "Write for Us" page, but its sidebar advertises the Adoovy guest post marketplace. Marketing Lad reported a $40 listing there, which we could not verify.

Is it safe to visit TheModelEats.com?

The homepage loaded normally in our checks on November 3 and November 8, 2026. As with any unfamiliar site, do not share login or payment information.

Our Verdict on TheModelEats.com

TheModelEats.com is an older domain, re-registered in 2024, that now hosts unrelated articles under a single marketplace-style byline and has not published since mid-2025. It is not a source we would rely on for legal, health or financial information, and we do not see a good case for paying to publish on it.
