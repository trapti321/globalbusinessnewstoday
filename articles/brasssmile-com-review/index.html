BrassSmile.com Review: A Dental Care Site or a Multi-Topic Blog? What We Verified

BrassSmile.com brands itself as dental care, but it is a 237-post blog on business, Diwali, gold and casinos. What we verified about the site.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 11, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

BrassSmile.com presents itself as a dental care and wellness site, but it is mainly a general multi-topic blog covering business, finance, Diwali, gold investing and online casinos. It offers no dental services, products or appointments. We checked its pages, its public post data, its domain record and its archive history on November 11, 2026. Its own Write for Us page bans casino posts, yet the site has published at least five.

BrassSmile.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a WordPress blog with 237 posts. Business (58 posts) is its biggest category; dental articles sit inside a smaller Health category.
  • Dental care? The site title says "Dental Care, Health Tips & Wellness Guides", but there is no dental practice, product or booking.
  • Domain: registered March 5, 2023. Early archived versions show no dental focus.
  • Contradiction: it says "No casino, CBD, adult, or link-placement articles" but has published casino bonus and Bitcoin casino posts.
  • Verdict: a content site. Do not rely on it for dental or financial advice.

What Is BrassSmile.com?

The homepage of BrassSmile.com is titled "BrassSmile.com | Dental Care, Health Tips & Wellness Guides", and its description promises "dental care advice" and "oral hygiene guides". The name suggests a dental clinic or teeth-whitening brand.

The content tells a different story. On November 11, 2026, its public WordPress data listed 237 posts. The largest categories were Business (58), Health (26), Education (23), Finance (21) and Blog (21), followed by Home, Technology, Lifestyle, Law, Fashion and Travel. There is no dental category. The site also has four simple calculator pages (GPA, fuel cost, compound interest and BMI), all added on October 17, 2026.

What BrassSmile.com Publishes

Its eight most recent posts, all by the byline "Shivi Hyde", focus on Indian festivals and investing:

  • "Diwali Sweets and Your Teeth" (November 10, 2026)
  • "After-Diwali Budget Reset" (November 9)
  • "10 Diwali Side Hustle Ideas" (November 5)
  • "Muhurat Trading 2026" (November 4)
  • "Physical Gold vs Digital Gold vs Gold ETF" (November 3)
  • Share price comparisons of ONGC and Reliance Industries (October 21)

The site also carries promotional posts for another website, SoSoActive, and a post simply titled "Backlinks" (December 2024).

Casino posts despite a casino ban

BrassSmile's Write for Us page says: "No casino, CBD, adult, or link-placement articles." Its archive includes:

  • "$3 Deposit Casino Bonuses in New Zealand" (July 9, 2026)
  • "A Comparative Analysis of Online Casino Free Spins and No Deposit Cash Bonuses" (June 10, 2026)
  • "What are the most trusted Bitcoin casino sites in 2026?" (March 19, 2026)
  • "How to play 918Kiss APK Senang" (March 19, 2026)
  • "What Michigan Players Should Know About Online Casinos in 2026" (February 13, 2026)

Gambling posts like these are commonly paid placements. None is labelled as sponsored.

Who Runs BrassSmile.com?

No owner, company or editor is named. There is no About page, and the site's /about-us/ and /contact-us/ addresses redirect to the homepage, so the Write for Us page's instruction to "Pitch us via the contact page" leads nowhere. Posts are credited to three accounts: "Shivi Hyde", "Devin Haney" and an account named after the site. The only contact is a personal Gmail address hidden in the page code.

Unlike SnapJotz.com and QuikConsole.com, BrassSmile does not use the shared Gmail account we have found across a network of guest-post blogs.

BrassSmile.com Domain History

  • March 5, 2023: domain registered through Namecheap, according to the Verisign .com registry.
  • August 2023: earliest posts, on content marketing, IT consulting and HIIT workouts.
  • December 2023: the Wayback Machine shows a generic blog titled "Brass Smile" with posts on QR codes and home buying. No dental focus.
  • 2026: dental branding, calculators and Diwali content added.

Is BrassSmile.com Legit for Dental Advice?

We found no scam reports, and the site does not ask readers for money. But it is not a dental authority:

  • Its authors have no stated dental qualifications.
  • Most of its content is unrelated to teeth.
  • It publishes unlabelled casino content its own rules prohibit.

For trustworthy dental information, use the CDC's oral health pages, the National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research or your own dentist.

Similar sites we have reviewed include MagazinePanda.com and Adstotally.com.

BrassSmile.com FAQs

Is BrassSmile a dental clinic?

No. It is a blog. It offers no dental treatment, products or appointments.

Who owns BrassSmile.com?

The site does not say. It has no About or working Contact page.

Does BrassSmile.com accept guest posts?

It has a Write for Us page, though its link to the contact page does not work.

When was BrassSmile.com created?

The domain was registered on March 5, 2023.

Checked on November 11, 2026, using the site's own pages and public WordPress data, the Verisign registry record and the Wayback Machine. We will update this review if BrassSmile.com responds.
