Press Release: Nexura Global Logistics Announces $45M Automated Fulfillment Center Expansion

Nexura Global Logistics breaks ground on a multi-modal distribution facility designed to streamline trans-Pacific cargo freight.

Corporate WirePress Distribution • Published Oct 31, 2026 • 3 min read
Share:

SYDNEY / NEW YORK — Corporate Wire — Nexura Global Logistics, a premier provider of international supply chain management solutions, has formally announced the groundbreaking of its new flagship automated distribution hub.

The 350,000 sq ft facility incorporates state-of-the-art robotic sorting racks, electric fleet charging bays, and automated customs clearance documentation pipelines.
