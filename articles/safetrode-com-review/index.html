Safetrode.com Review: What the Site Is, Who Runs It and Whether It Is Legit

Safetrode.com is a multi-topic WordPress blog, not a safety brand. We checked its domain records, archive history and sponsored links.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 15, 2026 • 4 min read
Share

Safetrode.com is a general-interest WordPress blog that posts articles on business, finance, technology, law, lifestyle and home improvement. It does not sell products, offer trading, or provide medical or electrical safety services. Our checks found no sign of a scam, but the site gives little detail about who runs it, and some of its articles carry commercial links without a visible sponsored label.

Safetrode.com at a glance:

  • What it is: A multi-topic blog that calls itself a "modern digital publication."
  • Platform: WordPress, behind Cloudflare.
  • Domain age: Registered February 13, 2019.
  • Ownership: Not named on the site; contact is a free Gmail address.
  • Verdict: Fine to read for general information, but not a source to rely on for money, legal or health decisions.

What Is Safetrode.com?

The homepage title reads "Safetrode.com | Trusted News, Insights & Expert Editorials," and the site header shows the name as "Safe Trode." Its menu lists seven sections: Business, Education, Finance, Home Improvement, Law, Lifestyle and Technology.

Some other websites describe it as a safety or electrode brand. The live site publishes general explainers, not product pages.

Who Runs Safetrode.com?

The site does not say. Its About page describes a mission and coverage areas but names no company, editor or address. The Contact page lists a single Gmail address. Posts on the homepage appear under one author account.

Public domain records do not fill the gap. The Verisign RDAP record shows the domain was registered on February 13, 2019, through Wild West Domains, LLC, and is set to expire on February 13, 2032. Registrant details are not shown.

Has the Domain Changed Hands?

Archived copies suggest so. The earliest Wayback Machine snapshot, from December 23, 2021, shows safetrode.com as the website of a Chennai, India, supplier of chemical earthing systems and lightning arresters. By April 2022, the same address showed a general blog titled "Safetrode, Daily Enjoyment with Daily News."

The current design came later. Its About, Contact, Disclaimer and Editorial Policy pages are all dated June 17, 2026. We found no evidence that the current blog is linked to the earthing business that once used the domain.

What Does It Publish?

The WordPress API reports 83 posts. Technology is the largest category with 27 posts, followed by Finance with 16. Recent headlines include a guide to multi-account futures trading, a South Carolina apostille explainer, an online casino safety checklist and a post on hiring Java developers.

Is Safetrode.com Legit and Safe?

It is a real, working blog. It asks for no payments, logins or personal data, so it looks low risk to browse.

Trust in what it publishes is a different question. The authors are not identified in any meaningful way, and the site's own Disclaimer says it "makes no guarantees" about accuracy. Treat its finance, legal and health posts as starting points, not advice.

Guest Posts and Sponsored Content

The sidebar shows a banner advertising a "Guest Post Marketplace," and the header shows another ad banner. Both route through link-shortening services. The Editorial Policy says sponsored content "will be clearly identified when applicable."

In two posts we opened, we found keyword links to a trade copier product and a casino free-spins page. Neither page showed a "sponsored" label that we could find. The FTC's Endorsement Guides FAQ says paid connections should be disclosed "clearly and conspicuously."

How Can You Check a Site Like This?

  • Look up the domain age and registrar with ICANN Lookup.
  • Check the About and Contact pages for a named company and physical address.
  • Search the Wayback Machine to see what the domain used to be.
  • Be wary of links that push you to trading, casino or download pages.
  • Never enter passwords or payment details from a blog link. The FTC's guide on recognizing phishing scams explains the warning signs.

FAQs

Is Safetrode.com a scam?

We found no evidence of a scam. It is an information blog that does not sell anything or ask for personal data.

Is Safetrode.com a medical or electrical safety company?

No. The live site is a general blog. The domain once belonged to an earthing equipment supplier in India, according to a 2021 archive, but the current content is unrelated.

Who owns Safetrode.com?

The site does not disclose an owner, and public domain records do not show registrant details.

Does Safetrode.com accept guest posts?

The site has no "write for us" page, but its Contact page invites advertising and partnership requests, and its sidebar advertises a guest post marketplace.
