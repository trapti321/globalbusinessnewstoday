Best Smartwatches of 2026: Apple Watch Series 12 vs Pixel Watch 5, Galaxy Watch 9 and Garmin

Compare the best smartwatches of 2026: Apple Watch Series 12, SE 3, Ultra 4, Pixel Watch 5, Galaxy Watch 9 and Garmin Venu 4 on price, battery and health.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 9, 2026 • 8 min read
Share

The best smartwatch for you depends mostly on your phone. iPhone owners should look at the Apple Watch Series 12. Android users have two strong choices in the Google Pixel Watch 5 and Samsung Galaxy Watch 9. If battery life and fitness tracking matter more than apps, Garmin's Venu 4 lasts days instead of hours. This guide compares the main 2026 models on price, battery, health features and compatibility.

Best smartwatches of 2026 at a glance:

  • Best for iPhone: Apple Watch Series 12, from $399
  • Best value for iPhone: Apple Watch SE 3, from $249 at launch
  • Best rugged and outdoor: Apple Watch Ultra 4, from $799
  • Best for Android and Pixel phones: Google Pixel Watch 5, from $399
  • Best for Samsung phones: Samsung Galaxy Watch 9, from $379.99
  • Best battery life for fitness: Garmin Venu 4, from $549.99

How we chose: we compared each manufacturer's published specifications and prices, and checked launch details in independent coverage, on November 9, 2026. We did not test these watches ourselves. Prices are US starting prices and change often.

1. Apple Watch Series 12: Best Smartwatch for iPhone

Announced on September 9, 2026, the Apple Watch Series 12 starts at $399 in 42mm and 46mm sizes. Its main upgrade is a new Health Sensing System with new optical and electrical heart sensors. Apple says it measures heart rate every five seconds all day and heart rate variability (HRV) up to 24 times more often. A new Readiness feature gives a daily 0 to 10 score with a recommendation for how hard to train.

  • Battery: up to 24 hours, and up to 10 hours of outdoor workout tracking, 25% more than before. A 15-minute charge adds up to 12 hours.
  • Chip: Apple's S11.
  • Best for: most iPhone owners who want the fullest mix of apps, payments, health and fitness tracking.
  • Watch out for: you still need to charge it most nights.

2. Apple Watch SE 3: Best Value for iPhone

The Apple Watch SE 3 is Apple's lowest-priced current model, in 40mm and 44mm aluminium cases with an always-on display. It runs on the S10 chip and includes sleep score, sleep apnea notifications, irregular rhythm notifications and wrist temperature sensing. It lacks ECG, blood oxygen, Readiness and hypertension notifications, according to Apple.

  • Battery: up to 18 hours, or 32 hours in Low Power Mode.
  • Best for: first-time smartwatch buyers, teenagers and anyone who mainly wants notifications and activity tracking.

3. Apple Watch Ultra 4: Best Rugged Smartwatch

The Apple Watch Ultra 4 has a 49mm titanium case, a sapphire display that reaches 3,000 nits, and military-standard (MIL-STD-810H) durability testing. It supports recreational scuba diving to 40 metres with a compatible app. It can also send emergency SOS, Find My and Messages over satellite when there is no mobile signal.

  • Battery: up to 50 hours, or 84 hours in Low Power Mode.
  • Price: from $799, the same as the Ultra 3, according to launch coverage.
  • Best for: hikers, divers, trail runners and anyone who wants the longest-lasting Apple Watch.

4. Google Pixel Watch 5: Best for Android and Pixel Phones

Launched on August 12, 2026, the Pixel Watch 5 comes in 41mm ($399 Wi-Fi, $499 LTE) and 45mm ($429 Wi-Fi, $529 LTE) sizes, Droid Life reported. It runs Wear OS with Google's Gemini assistant, which can handle some voice commands offline. It also adds satellite SOS.

  • Health and safety: irregular rhythm, high and low heart rate, breathing emergency and loss-of-pulse alerts, plus a Readiness Score and improved sleep tracking.
  • Battery: about 30 hours (41mm) or 40 hours (45mm) with the always-on display, and longer in battery saver mode.
  • Best for: Pixel and other Android phone owners who want strong safety features and Fitbit-based fitness tracking.

5. Samsung Galaxy Watch 9: Best for Samsung Phones

Samsung announced the Galaxy Watch 9 on July 22, 2026. It starts at $379.99 for the 40mm Bluetooth model, $30 more than last year. It uses Qualcomm's Snapdragon Wear Elite chip and runs One UI 9 Watch, and both sizes get bigger batteries than the Galaxy Watch 8: 390mAh (40mm) and 445mAh (45mm), according to SamMobile.

  • Display: up to 3,000 nits peak brightness.
  • Best for: Galaxy phone owners. Some health features work best, or only, with a Samsung phone.
  • Watch out for: reviewers describe it as an incremental upgrade, so Galaxy Watch 8 owners may not need to switch.

6. Garmin Venu 4: Best Battery Life for Fitness

The Garmin Venu 4, launched in September 2025 from $549.99, trades some smartwatch apps for battery life measured in days. Garmin says it lasts up to 12 days in smartwatch mode. It includes an ECG app that can detect signs of atrial fibrillation, a Health Status feature that flags when overnight metrics move outside your normal range, lifestyle logging for habits such as caffeine and alcohol, and a built-in LED flashlight.

  • Sizes: 41mm and 45mm.
  • Best for: runners and anyone who hates daily charging. It works with both iPhone and Android.

How to Choose the Right Smartwatch

  1. Start with your phone. Apple Watches only work with iPhone. Pixel and Galaxy watches need Android. Garmin works with both.
  2. Decide how often you will charge. Most smartwatches need charging every one to two days, while Garmin and the Apple Watch Ultra 4 last much longer.
  3. Pick the health features you will actually use. ECG, readiness scores and sleep tracking are useful, but not every model has every feature.
  4. Consider LTE. A cellular model lets you call and stream without your phone, but costs more upfront and usually needs a monthly plan.
  5. Check the fit. Smaller cases are lighter and often more comfortable for sleep tracking.

If you are buying a watch to support a new exercise habit, read our evidence-based guide to the benefits and risks of running every day.

A Note on Health Features

Smartwatch health alerts can prompt you to see a doctor, but they cannot diagnose conditions. Features such as ECG and irregular rhythm notifications have specific approvals and limitations that vary by country. If your watch flags a problem, or you feel unwell, contact a medical professional.

Best Smartwatch FAQs

What is the best smartwatch in 2026?

For iPhone users, the Apple Watch Series 12. For Android, the Google Pixel Watch 5 or Samsung Galaxy Watch 9, depending on your phone. For the longest battery life, the Garmin Venu 4.

Which smartwatch has the best battery life?

Of the watches here, the Garmin Venu 4, at up to 12 days in smartwatch mode. Among Apple models, the Ultra 4 lasts up to 50 hours.

Can I use an Apple Watch with an Android phone?

No. The Apple Watch requires an iPhone.

Are smartwatches worth it?

They are most useful if you will use the fitness, safety or notification features daily. If you only want step counts, a cheaper fitness tracker may be enough.
