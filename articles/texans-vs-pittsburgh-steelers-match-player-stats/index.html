Texans vs Pittsburgh Steelers Match Player Stats: Full Box Score From Houston's 30-6 Wild Card Win

Texans vs Steelers match player stats from the 30-6 AFC Wild Card on Jan 12, 2026: Stroud, Rodgers, Kirk, Marks, sacks, scoring plays and team totals.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 8, 2026 • 6 min read
Share

The Houston Texans beat the Pittsburgh Steelers 30-6 in the AFC Wild Card round on Monday, January 12, 2026, at Acrisure Stadium in Pittsburgh. It was the most recent Texans vs Steelers game. Below are the full match player stats: passing, rushing, receiving and defence, checked against the ESPN box score and the Steelers' official game page.

Texans vs Steelers at a glance: Texans 30, Steelers 6 · AFC Wild Card · January 12, 2026 · Acrisure Stadium, Pittsburgh · Attendance 67,297. Top performers: Christian Kirk (8 catches, 144 yards, 1 TD), Woody Marks (112 rushing yards, 1 TD), Sheldon Rankins (1.5 sacks and a fumble-return TD).

Houston scored 23 unanswered points in the fourth quarter, including two defensive touchdowns. It was the first road playoff win in Texans franchise history and the Steelers' seventh straight playoff loss under head coach Mike Tomlin. The teams meet again on December 6, 2026, at Acrisure Stadium. For other recent playoff box scores, see our Pistons vs Magic Game 7 player stats.

What the Stats Show

The game was close for three quarters: Houston led only 7-6 entering the fourth. The difference was Pittsburgh's offence, which managed 175 total yards and converted 2 of 14 third downs. Houston's defence then turned the game with two touchdowns in under nine minutes.

"We're here for it all," Rankins said after the game, according to the Associated Press. "We're here for the whole thing." Tomlin said, "It's the here and now, and certainly it's difficult. But that's what we sign up for." Rodgers, asked about his future, said: "I'm not going to make any emotional decisions. I'm disappointed."

The win improved Houston to 13-5 and extended its winning streak to 10 games, sending the Texans to New England for the Divisional Round on January 18. Pittsburgh, the AFC North champion, finished 10-8.
