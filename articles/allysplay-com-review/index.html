Allysplay.com Review: What the Gaming Blog Really Is, Who Runs It and Is It Safe

Allysplay.com is a gaming blog, not a game platform or app. We checked its domain records and archive history, bylines, off-topic posts and safety.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 11, 2026 • 4 min read
Share

Allysplay.com is a WordPress gaming blog with news, reviews, guides and cheat codes. It is not a game store, a streaming service or a betting platform, and it has no app or account system. We checked it on November 11, 2026. Its domain was registered on April 12, 2024, not in 2023 as some reviews suggest, and since mid-2026 it has blocked automated visitors behind a Cloudflare check.

Allysplay.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a gaming blog with sections for Console Gaming, Game News, Game Reviews, Kids' Games, Game Guides and Game Mods & Cheats.
  • Domain: registered April 12, 2024, through Hosting Ukraine LLC, according to the Verisign .com registry.
  • Byline: posts are credited to "Steven Molly", published from a single admin account.
  • Off-topic posts: payments, scholarships and men's suits, at least one with a followed commercial link.
  • Verdict: a low-risk blog to read, but verify codes, dates and mod downloads elsewhere.

What Is Allysplay.com?

Allysplay.com uses the homepage title "Level Up Your Gaming Knowledge with Our Premier Game Blog!" Archived copies from December 2024 through May 2026 show the same layout: short news posts about trailers and release dates, longer review-style pieces, walkthroughs such as "Resident Evil 5 Walkthrough With Beginner Tips", tier lists and cheat-code roundups for games such as The Sims 4 and Medieval II: Total War.

There is nothing to download, stream or buy on the site. Some ranking pages, such as a February 2026 review on timeinus.com, describe "payment methods", "checkout" and "account management". We found no evidence of any of these on Allysplay.com, and they appear to be generic text written for a different kind of platform.

Allysplay.com History

  • April 12, 2024: domain registered through Hosting Ukraine LLC. The registry record was last changed on March 30, 2026, and the domain expires on April 12, 2027.
  • April 22, 2024: the first Wayback Machine capture shows only a Russian-language hosting placeholder ("Congratulations, the site is created!").
  • December 14, 2024: the gaming blog is live, with guides marked as "5 months ago", which points to content first posted around mid-2024.
  • 2025: the site keeps adding game news, guides and tier lists, plus a few unrelated posts.
  • July 2026 onward: archive crawlers and our own requests receive a Cloudflare 403 challenge page instead of the site.

The Buzzsport review says the homepage was "first published in September 2023". The homepage code does carry a September 27, 2023 date, but that is seven months before the domain existed, so it most likely came from a theme or imported template, not from the site's real launch. We found no sign of an earlier owner.

Who Runs Allysplay.com?

No company or legal owner is named. Posts carry the byline "Steven Molly", but in the archived pages they all link to the same author account, "admin". The archived contact page lists an email on the site's own domain, not a Gmail address, and it is not the Gmail account shared by the guest-post network behind SparkPressFusion, SnapJotz and QuikConsole.

Off-Topic and Commercial Posts

Most of Allysplay.com is about games, but the archive shows posts that are not, all credited to the same account:

Posts like these are typical of blogs that sell guest posts or links. They do not make the gaming content false, but they mean not every post was written for gamers.

Is Allysplay.com Safe?

From what we could check, reading Allysplay.com is low-risk. It uses HTTPS, has Privacy and Cookie Policy pages, and does not require an account or payment to read posts. We saw no gambling, loan or CBD promotion on the archived homepage from May 2026.

Three cautions apply. First, cheat and redemption codes expire, so check the post date and redeem codes only inside the official game. Second, posts about mods are not downloads; get mods only from official or well-known mod hubs and scan files before opening them. Third, the Cloudflare check may ask you to verify that you are human. That is normal for Cloudflare, but never install software or allow notifications to pass such a check. For general advice on risky downloads, see the FTC's malware guide.

Allysplay.com FAQs

Is Allysplay.com a gaming platform where I can play games?

No. It is a blog. You read articles there; you cannot play, buy or stream games.

When was Allysplay.com created?

The domain was registered on April 12, 2024, and the blog was live by December 2024.

Who writes for Allysplay.com?

Posts are credited to "Steven Molly", but no biography or owner is published.

Does Allysplay.com have an app?

We found no official app. Avoid any download that claims to be one.

Checked on November 11, 2026, against the Verisign registry record, Wayback Machine captures of Allysplay.com from April 2024 to May 2026, and the top-ranking reviews of the site. The live site returned a Cloudflare 403 page to our requests. We will update this review if Allysplay.com responds.
