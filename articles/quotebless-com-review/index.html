QuoteBless.com USA Review: From Free Blessings to Unlabelled Paid-Style Posts

QuoteBless.com began as a free blessings and quotes site. Since February 2026, most of its posts are unlabelled outside articles. Here is what we verified.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 11, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

QuoteBless.com started in August 2025 as a free site of daily blessings, quotes and copy-ready text replies. Since February 2026 most of its new posts have been on unrelated topics such as trading apps, cosmetic surgery, medical marijuana and law firms. We checked the site on November 11, 2026. Of its 178 posts, 104 were published from February 13, 2026 onward, and only a handful of those are quotes or blessings. Its contact email is on the domain of GP IT Firm, a guest-posting and link-building agency.

QuoteBless.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a WordPress site with 178 posts: blessings, quotes, "responses" to texts, and a growing number of outside articles.
  • Is it free? Yes. Quotes can be copied and images downloaded for personal, non-commercial use.
  • Domain: registered August 17, 2025, through NameCheap, Inc.
  • Is it US-based? No US address or company is listed.
  • Verdict: its older quote pages are harmless. Its newer posts read like paid placements, with no sponsorship labels.

What Is QuoteBless.com?

QuoteBless.com calls itself "Quotes and Blessings to Brighten Everyone's Day". Its About page describes "a place where words and images come together to inspire, uplift, and bring joy into everyday life", with a copy button on each message and free image downloads that it asks readers not to use commercially.

Its early content matches that description. Between August 19 and October 5, 2025, it published near-daily lists such as "200+ Friday Blessings to Brighten Your Day", "340+ Happy Birthday Blessings for Every Heart and Moment" and "How Are You Responses: 220+ Perfect Copy-Ready Text Replies". From November 2025 to January 2026 it added a smaller number of posts on how to reply to texts.

How QuoteBless.com Changed in 2026

On February 13, 2026, the site published "The Role of Voice Innovation in Inclusive Design" and a biography of footballer Theo De Percin. Since then, new posts have been mostly unrelated to quotes. Monthly output peaked at 34 posts in September 2026.

We counted 181 outbound links in the 104 posts published since February 13, 2026. None carried a "nofollow" or "sponsored" label. Google's spam policies say paid links should be labelled that way. The site's category list now includes Business, CBD, Law, Real Estate and Tech News alongside Blessings and Quotes.

We have seen the same shift on other sites searched with "usa", such as TozyMagazine.com, which moved from birthday wishes to commercial posts, and SpiritualHubz.com, a spiritual blog whose newest posts drifted into commercial topics.

Who Runs QuoteBless.com?

The About page names "Umer" as editor. The site's main WordPress account is named "Dilawar Mughal", and its profile describes him as "the creator and voice behind QuoteBless" before calling him "Umer" in the next sentence. Two other accounts, "mass tamilan" and "Alfred Kennedy", are credited with one recent post each.

The Contact page lists no address on the site's own domain. Its email address is on gpitfirm.com, the website of GP IT Firm, which describes itself as offering "Guest Posting & Link Building SEO services" and "link insertion". No company name, country or street address appears on QuoteBless. We did not find the Gmail address used by the network behind SparkPressFusion and Domikyo.

QuoteBless.com Domain History

  • August 17, 2025: domain registered through NameCheap, Inc., according to the Verisign .com registry. The homepage and privacy policy were created the same day.
  • March 27, 2025 date: one post, "Signs Your Home Has Poor Drainage", is dated before the domain existed, so it was backdated or imported.
  • October 26, 2025: earliest Wayback Machine snapshot, showing the same quotes-and-blessings title.
  • February 13, 2026: first posts on unrelated topics.

Is QuoteBless.com Safe?

The site asks for no money or account, and its older quote and blessing lists are what they claim to be. We found no scam reports. Be careful with its newer articles: they promote trading platforms, cosmetic surgery and cannabis services without disclosing whether they were paid for. For health or money decisions, rely on a doctor, a licensed adviser or official sources rather than these posts. If you share a quote credited to a famous person, check the attribution first.

QuoteBless.com FAQs

Is QuoteBless.com free?

Yes. Copying quotes and downloading images is free for personal use.

Can I use QuoteBless images commercially?

No. The About page asks readers not to use them for commercial purposes.

Is QuoteBless.com a US website?

It lists no US address or company. The "usa" in searches does not point to a separate US site.

Does QuoteBless.com accept guest posts?

It has no Write for Us page, but its contact email belongs to a guest-posting agency and most of its 2026 posts are outside articles.

When was QuoteBless.com created?

The domain was registered on August 17, 2025.
