IncaBizGrowth.com Review: A Rebranded 2017 Blog, Not a Business Growth Service

IncaBizGrowth.com is a rebranded 2017 blog with 110 posts, mostly old health content, not a growth service. A separate .site domain recruits paid reviewers.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 11, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

IncaBizGrowth.com is a WordPress blog, not a business growth service or agency. We checked it on November 11, 2026. It lists 110 posts, most of them old health and lifestyle articles, and it rebranded as a business blog in autumn 2026. A separate site, incabizgrowth.site, registered in August 2026, uses the same name to recruit paid "product reviewers", and readers should not confuse the two.

IncaBizGrowth.com at a glance:

  • What it is: a multi-topic WordPress blog with 110 posts. Health (23) and Business (21) are its biggest categories.
  • Is it a growth agency? No. It sells no services. It offers four free calculators added in October 2026.
  • Domain: registered April 18, 2017, through Namecheap, according to the Verisign .com registry.
  • Network link: its contact addresses are not the Gmail account shared by the guest-post blogs we have reviewed.
  • Watch out for: incabizgrowth.site, a different domain offering up to "$750/month" for product reviews.

What Is IncaBizGrowth.com?

IncaBizGrowth.com calls itself a "Business, Marketing, Tech & Finance Blog". Its About page says "Inca Biz Growth" is "a dedicated business blog" for entrepreneurs, founders and marketers, and that its content is "focused entirely on the business world".

The archive does not match that description. On November 11, 2026, the site's public WordPress data listed 110 posts across 28 categories. Health is the largest category with 23 posts, ahead of Business (21), Blog (20), Finance (7), Education (6) and Technology (6). Only 15 of the 110 posts are dated 2026. The rest include topics such as teeth whitening, heart bypass recovery, cosmetic surgery, mobile games and "Is It legal to use cannabis oil".

A 2017 Domain With a 2026 Rebrand

The Wayback Machine shows how the site changed over time:

DateWhat the homepage showed
April 21, 2017"Inc A Biz Growth", a default WordPress demo theme with "Sample Page"
June 27, 2020"Inca Biz Growth", a general news blog covering health, travel and automobiles
July 14, 2026A magazine theme still showing template text about "creative news from FooBar"
November 11, 2026"Practical business growth", with free calculators and new legal pages

The About page was created on September 12, 2026, and the Contact, Write for Us, Advertise, Editorial Policy, legal and calculator pages were all created on October 17, 2026. The registry record was last changed on April 30, 2026.

Some dates have also been rewritten. The 2020 archive shows the post "How can a woman look more attractive?" dated September 22, 2019. The live site now dates it December 13, 2021, the same date given to 15 different health and lifestyle posts.

Who Runs IncaBizGrowth.com?

No owner, company or address is named. The About page describes "a team of business writers, marketers, and industry researchers" but names nobody. Of the 110 posts, 87 are credited to an account called "itsdevin" and 19 to "Shivi Hyde". The Editorial Policy page still contains unfinished template text in square brackets: "[describe how you research and test what you write]".

The homepage and Contact page list two different Gmail addresses. Neither is the Gmail account shared by SparkPressFusion, Domikyo, Adstotally, SnapJotz and QuikConsole, and we found no "mansi" byline. We found no evidence that IncaBizGrowth.com belongs to that network.

Guest Post Rules vs. the Archive

The Write for Us page says the site accepts "No casino, CBD, adult, or link-placement articles", and the Advertise page says it does not "sell undisclosed links". Older posts still on the site include "Unveiling the Power of CBD Isolate" (November 2023), "Important information about traveling with sex toys" (August 2023) and a December 2024 post titled "Backlinks" that is a list of 318 links, each labeled only "Link". Other posts promote similarly named sites such as Picnob, Urlebird, Blookle and Wallpostmedia, a common pattern on link-selling blogs like TozyMagazine and UploadBlog.

The site also hosts medical content without named reviewers, including a 2021-dated post titled "Abortion Made Easy at Home With Natural Remedies". For health decisions, use official sources such as MedlinePlus or a doctor.

IncaBizGrowth.site Is a Different Website

A separate domain, incabizgrowth.site, was registered through Namecheap on August 28, 2026, according to its registry record. It titles itself "IncaBizGrowth.com | Become a Product Reviewer & Earn Rewards", promises "up to $750/month", claims "50,000+ reviewers" and shows first-name testimonials. We found nothing linking it to IncaBizGrowth.com, which does not mention it. We could not verify any of its claims. Never pay a fee to unlock earnings, and report suspicious job offers to the FTC.

What Other Reviews Get Wrong

A September 17, 2026, review on Buzzsport correctly calls IncaBizGrowth.com a content site and flags the .site domain. It says the registry record was updated in January 2026, but the record we checked shows April 30, 2026. It does not mention the rewritten post dates, the template text in the editorial policy or the CBD and adult posts that contradict the site's own rules.

Is IncaBizGrowth.com Safe?

We found no scam reports, and IncaBizGrowth.com does not ask readers for money. Reading it is low-risk. Treat it as an unedited blog: its business articles from September and October 2026 are newer, but its older health, finance and crypto posts are outdated and unreviewed.

IncaBizGrowth.com FAQs

Is IncaBizGrowth.com a business growth company?

No. It is a blog. It sells no consulting, marketing or funding services.

When was IncaBizGrowth.com created?

The domain was registered on April 18, 2017. Its current business-blog design dates from autumn 2026.

Does IncaBizGrowth.com accept guest posts?

Yes. Its Write for Us page invites pitches on startups, marketing, finance and leadership.

Is incabizgrowth.site the same website?

No. It is a different domain, registered in August 2026, that recruits paid product reviewers.

Checked on November 11, 2026, against the site's own pages and public WordPress data, the Verisign and .site registry records and the Wayback Machine. We will update this review if IncaBizGrowth.com responds.
