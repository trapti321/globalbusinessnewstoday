Camocrypt.com Review: Who Runs This Crypto Blog and Is It Safe?

Camocrypt.com is a WordPress crypto and NFT blog registered in December 2025. We checked its posts, guest post policy, ownership and safety.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 15, 2026 • 4 min read
Share

Camocrypt.com is a WordPress blog that publishes beginner guides and news-style posts about cryptocurrency, NFTs, blockchain and Web3. It does not sell a token or ask visitors to connect a wallet on its homepage, but it accepts guest posts, gives little information about who runs it, and also carries some casino and app download content.

Camocrypt.com at a glance:

  • What it is: A crypto and blockchain blog built on WordPress.
  • Size: 359 published posts as of November 15, 2026.
  • Domain registered: December 21, 2025, according to Verisign records.
  • Ownership: Not disclosed. There is no About page.
  • Guest posts: Yes. The homepage invites guest post requests.
  • Investment offers: None seen on the homepage. No token sale or wallet connect prompt.

What Is Camocrypt.com?

Camocrypt.com describes itself in its footer as a source for the latest crypto news, blockchain updates, price analysis and insights on Bitcoin, altcoins, NFTs and DeFi. The homepage title reads "Home News - CamoCrypt.com."

Interest in the name has grown after other blogs began publishing articles that mention it. One such page on Buzzsport, dated October 16, 2026, is a hot wallet versus cold wallet explainer presented as a guide "from camocrypt.com."

What It Publishes

The site's public WordPress data lists 359 posts. Recent posts appear roughly once a day and are mostly beginner explainers, such as guides to NFT minting, NFT gas fees, NFT marketplaces, Web3 and decentralized finance. The largest categories are Cryptocurrency, Crypto, Bitcoin and Cryptocurrency Trading.

Not everything is about crypto. A search of the site's posts returned six results for "casino," including no deposit bonus and slot bonus articles, and one guide to downloading a Malaysian casino app as an APK file that links to an outside download site.

The oldest post is dated March 18, 2022. That is earlier than the current domain registration, and we could not confirm whether older posts were moved from somewhere else. Of the 359 posts, 335 are dated after December 21, 2025.

Who Runs It?

The site does not say. The /about/ and /about-us/ addresses return "page not found," and we found no privacy policy at /privacy-policy/. The footer shows the default credit of its WordPress news theme rather than a company name.

The site's author list includes a general "admin" account, which wrote the recent daily posts, plus several accounts whose names refer to SEO, backlink and press release services. The contact page lists social media links, and the guest post contact address on the homepage uses a different domain from camocrypt.com.

Domain records list Hostinger as the registrar. Owner details are not public in the registry data we checked.

Guest Posts and Sponsored Content

The homepage carries a "Contact For Guest Posts" notice. Readers should treat any post that names a specific exchange, app, casino or coin as possibly promotional unless the site states otherwise.

Is Camocrypt.com Safe?

We found no token sale, investment plan, wallet connect button or crypto payment request on the homepage. A web search on November 15, 2026, also turned up no public scam warnings that name the site. That does not make it an authority.

The main risks are about trust, not malware. Ownership is not disclosed, the domain is under a year old in its current registration, the site accepts guest posts, and some posts cover gambling and side-loaded app downloads. Use it for background reading at most, and check any financial claim against an official or established source.

How to Check Gaming and Crypto Sites

  • Look for an About page, a named publisher and a real contact method.
  • Check the domain's age with ICANN Lookup.
  • Never send crypto to anyone who promises guaranteed returns. The FTC's guide What To Know About Cryptocurrency and Scams explains common warning signs.
  • Read the CFTC's alert on fraudulent digital asset trading websites before using any trading platform.
  • Report suspected fraud at ReportFraud.ftc.gov or the FBI's IC3.

FAQs

Is camocrypt.com a crypto exchange?

No. It is a blog. We saw no trading, deposit or wallet features on its homepage.

Who owns camocrypt.com?

The site does not name an owner, and registry records do not show one publicly.

How old is camocrypt.com?

The current registration dates from December 21, 2025, though some posts carry dates back to 2022.

Can I trust its investment content?

Treat it as general reading only. The site is not a licensed adviser, and guest posts may be promotional.

Checked on November 15, 2026, against camocrypt.com and its public WordPress data, Verisign RDAP records, the Internet Archive Wayback Machine, and guidance from the FTC and CFTC.
