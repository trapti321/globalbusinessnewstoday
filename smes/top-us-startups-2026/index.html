Top US Startups to Watch in 2026: 17 Companies Ranked by Valuation

Top US startups in 2026, from OpenAI and Databricks to Ramp, Kalshi and Harvey, ranked by verified valuation, plus Anthropic's IPO and SpaceX's buys.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 15, 2026 • 7 min read
Share

The top startups in the United States in 2026 are led by AI companies. OpenAI ($852 billion), Databricks ($190 billion), Stripe ($159 billion), Anduril ($61 billion) and Ramp ($60 billion) top our list of 17 private US startups ranked by their latest confirmed valuation. Anthropic is about to leave the list through a stock market listing, and two former leaders, xAI and Cursor maker Anysphere, were bought by SpaceX this year. Every figure below was checked against company announcements, regulatory filings or major outlets as of November 15, 2026.

US startups in 2026, the key facts:

  • Most valuable private startup: OpenAI, at $852 billion after a $122 billion round announced in March 2026.
  • Biggest IPO in progress: Anthropic, with pricing expected on November 18 and trading on Nasdaq under ANT.
  • Record funding: US startups raised $515.8 billion in the first nine months of 2026, according to the PitchBook-NVCA Venture Monitor.
  • AI dominance: AI companies took 82.7% of US venture deal value through September.
  • Fastest climbers: Ramp, Kalshi, Cognition and Mercor all raised at sharply higher valuations in 2026.

How We Ranked the Top US Startups

We ranked privately held, venture-backed companies headquartered in the United States by their latest valuation that has been confirmed by the company or reported by a major outlet. Where a newer round has been reported but not confirmed, we show the confirmed figure and note the report. Companies that were acquired or are in the final stage of an IPO are listed separately. Valuations are a snapshot of what investors paid, not a measure of profit, and private valuations can fall as well as rise.

AI Model Makers

1. OpenAI

OpenAI raised $122 billion at an $852 billion post-money valuation in a round announced at the end of March 2026, anchored by Amazon, Nvidia and SoftBank. At the time it reported about $2 billion of revenue a month and more than 900 million weekly ChatGPT users. CNBC reported in November that OpenAI is raising about $15 billion more at a $1.25 trillion valuation; the company has not announced it. OpenAI submitted a confidential draft IPO filing in June and has said a listing would come in 2027.

Anthropic (IPO pending)

Anthropic, maker of Claude, raised $65 billion at a $965 billion valuation in May 2026, CNBC reported. Its public IPO filing shows $33.2 billion of revenue in the first nine months of 2026, and it plans to sell shares at $70 to $78 each, with pricing expected on November 18. Read our coverage of the Anthropic IPO filing.

9. Safe Superintelligence and 14. Thinking Machines Lab

Two research labs founded by former OpenAI leaders have no mass-market product yet but command large valuations. Ilya Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence was last valued at a reported $32 billion in April 2025, and Reuters reported in July 2026 that Nvidia would invest $5 billion. Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab raised about $2 billion at $12 billion in 2025 and was reported in November 2026 to be near a round at a $40 billion pre-money valuation led by Accel, which it has not confirmed.

AI Applications and Developer Tools

2. Databricks

Databricks raised $5 billion at a $190 billion valuation in August 2026 and said its revenue run-rate passed $7 billion, growing more than 80% a year, according to its press release.

6. Cognition

Cognition, maker of the Devin coding agent and owner of Windsurf, raised more than $2 billion at a $48 billion valuation in September 2026, led by a16z and Accel. It said run-rate revenue rose from $492 million in May to almost $900 million in September, according to its announcement.

10. Mercor, 12. Sierra, 13. Harvey and 15. Glean

  • Mercor raised $750 million at $20 billion on November 10, 2026, with Nvidia, Salesforce and Benchmark among investors, SiliconANGLE reported. It disclosed a security incident earlier in the year.
  • Sierra, co-founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor, raised a $950 million Series E at $15.8 billion in May 2026, led by Tiger Global and GV.
  • Harvey raised $550 million at $15.5 billion in September 2026 and says 80% of the Am Law 100 law firms use it.
  • Glean passed $300 million in annual recurring revenue in May 2026; its last confirmed valuation is $7.2 billion from June 2025.

11. Perplexity

Perplexity's last widely reported valuation was $20 billion in September 2025. The Information reported in August 2026 that Nvidia was in talks to invest at more than $30 billion, but no close has been confirmed.

Fintech and Prediction Markets

3. Stripe

Stripe was valued at $159 billion in an employee share sale in February 2026 and processed $1.9 trillion of total volume in 2025, up 34%, according to its annual letter. In August it agreed to buy AI model marketplace OpenRouter.

5. Ramp

Ramp raised $1.85 billion at a $60 billion valuation in October 2026, led by Dragoneer, up from $44 billion in June. It reported more than $2 billion of annualized revenue and 85,000 customers, TechCrunch reported.

7. Kalshi and 16. Polymarket

Prediction markets were one of 2026's breakout sectors. Kalshi, a CFTC-regulated exchange, raised $1 billion at $40 billion on October 29, co-led by Sequoia and Wellington Management, almost double its May valuation. Polymarket reported annualized revenue above $1.8 billion in November; a round at $21 billion led by 1789 Capital was reported in August but had not been confirmed as closed. Both face legal challenges from states over sports contracts.

Defense, Robotics and Energy

  • 4. Anduril raised a $5 billion Series H at $61 billion in May 2026, led by Thrive Capital and a16z, after 2025 revenue of $2.2 billion. In October it announced a $6.6 billion submarine shipyard package with the US Navy.
  • 8. Figure AI was valued at $39 billion in its September 2025 Series C and said in April 2026 that it had delivered more than 350 Figure 03 humanoid robots.
  • 17. Commonwealth Fusion Systems raised about $1 billion in July 2026, with Google and Eni among investors, bringing total funding to $4 billion. It aims to switch on its SPARC fusion machine in 2027.

Which Big Startups Exited in 2026?

Two of the most valuable US startups were absorbed by SpaceX, which itself listed on Nasdaq in June 2026.

  • xAI: Elon Musk's AI company raised $20 billion in January, then was acquired by SpaceX in an all-stock deal in February that valued xAI at $250 billion, CNBC reported.
  • Anysphere (Cursor): SpaceX completed its purchase of the AI coding company on August 14, 2026, at an implied equity value of $60 billion, according to SpaceX's SEC filing.

What the 2026 Startup Market Looks Like

US venture investment hit a record $515.8 billion in the first three quarters of 2026, about 44% above the previous full-year record set in 2021, according to the PitchBook-NVCA Venture Monitor. AI companies took 82.7% of that money. Third-quarter investment was $98.4 billion across an estimated 5,012 deals, and 18 venture-backed companies went public in the quarter.

The concentration is striking: a handful of AI model makers absorb most of the capital, while smaller startups compete for the rest. For founders, that means investors are paying up for AI growth but scrutinising everyone else. See our guides on venture debt and startup fundraising strategy.

FAQs

What is the most valuable startup in the United States?

OpenAI, valued at $852 billion after a round announced in March 2026. A newer round at $1.25 trillion has been reported but not announced by the company.

Which US startups are planning IPOs?

Anthropic is set to price its IPO on November 18, 2026. OpenAI has filed confidentially and expects to list in 2027, and Kalshi's CEO has said an IPO would come no earlier than 2027.

How much did US startups raise in 2026?

$515.8 billion through September 2026, a record, according to PitchBook and the NVCA, with 82.7% going to AI companies.

Is Cursor still a startup?

No. Cursor's maker, Anysphere, became part of SpaceX when the acquisition closed on August 14, 2026.
