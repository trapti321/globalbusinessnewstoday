About GBNT Newsroom

GBNT Newsroom is the in-house editorial desk of Global Business News Today. The desk researches, writes and fact-checks business news and explainers for executives, founders, investors and small business owners who need clear answers backed by evidence.

Stories published under the GBNT Newsroom byline are produced and reviewed by the editorial team as a whole rather than by a single reporter. Each one goes through a source check before publication, and the desk returns to explainers when the facts change, recording the update date on the article.

Coverage Areas

  • Tech & AI
  • SMEs & Startups

How We Report

  • Primary sources first. Figures and claims are taken from regulatory filings, central bank statements, official statistics, court records and company announcements, and linked inline so readers can check them.
  • Clear dates. Every article shows when it was published and, where relevant, when it was last updated or when its facts were last checked.
  • Separation of news and promotion. Press releases are published only in the Press Releases section and are labelled as corporate announcements.
  • Corrections. Errors are corrected promptly. Readers can report a mistake through the contact page or by emailing info@globalbusinessnewstoday.com.

Read our full editorial standards and corrections policy.
