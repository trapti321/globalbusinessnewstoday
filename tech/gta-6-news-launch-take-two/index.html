GTA 6 News: Grand Theft Auto VI Launches Nov 19 as Take-Two Says Development Is Complete

GTA 6 news: Grand Theft Auto VI arrives Nov 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S at $79.99, with 212GB pre-loads live and Take-Two keeping an $8B forecast.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 16, 2026 • 5 min read
Share
Add us as a preferred source on Google ››

The biggest GTA 6 news is that Grand Theft Auto VI launches on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Take-Two Interactive says development is finished, pre-loading opened on November 9 and 10, and the company has kept its full-year forecast of $8 billion to $8.2 billion in net bookings, a figure that depends largely on this one game.

GTA 6 news, the key facts:

  • Release date: November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S (Take-Two, November 9, 2026).
  • Price: $79.99 Standard Edition, $99.99 Ultimate Edition (announced June 2026).
  • Pre-load: Live since November 9 (Xbox) and November 10 (PS5); the download is up to 212GB.
  • PC: No PC version has been announced by Rockstar.
  • Development: Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said on November 9 that Rockstar "has completed the development" of the game.
  • Guidance: Fiscal 2027 net bookings outlook of $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion, reiterated.
  • Stock: Take-Two shares were up 7.5% at 1:32 p.m. ET on November 10, 2026, after earnings.

Why is GTA 6 news trending right now?

Three things landed within a week. On November 9, 2026, Take-Two Interactive (Nasdaq: TTWO), Rockstar Games' parent company, reported its fiscal second-quarter results and confirmed the November 19 date. On that day's earnings call, Zelnick said development was complete, which gaming sites reported as the game "going gold" (the industry term for a finished build ready for release). Pre-loading opened the same night. Then on November 14, CNBC reported that preorders had passed 12 million, citing Sensor Tower data. With launch days away, searches for release times, prices and file sizes have jumped.

Has GTA 6 really gone gold?

The phrase comes from the media rather than from Take-Two. According to Beebom's report on the November 9 earnings call, Zelnick said: "Right now, Rockstar has completed the development of GTA 6." He added, "That may not come as a surprise to you since we're 10 days away from the release." Neither Take-Two nor Rockstar used the word "gold" in an announcement we could find. He also said Rockstar's developers "have done their very best work, and it's going to come to consumers soon." In practice, a finished build and live pre-loads mean another delay is very unlikely.

What did Take-Two say in its earnings release?

Take-Two's fiscal second-quarter 2027 release, filed with the SEC on November 9, 2026, covers the quarter that ended September 30. Zelnick said: "The launch of Grand Theft Auto VI is ten days away and, with pre-orders continuing to reach unprecedented levels, we expect to deliver an historic year for our company."

The quarter itself came before the launch, so the numbers were weak:

GAAP net revenue$1.50 billion$1.77 billion

Looking ahead, Take-Two forecast third-quarter net bookings (October to December, which covers the launch) of $3.35 billion to $3.5 billion. For the full fiscal year ending March 2027, it reiterated net bookings of $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion, GAAP net revenue of $7.98 billion to $8.18 billion and GAAP diluted earnings of $0.57 to $0.75 per share.

How did Take-Two stock react?

Investors looked past the weak quarter. The Motley Fool reported that on November 10, 2026, Take-Two shares rose as much as 10.6% and were up 7.5% at 1:32 p.m. ET. The quarterly loss of $0.29 per share was smaller than the $0.86 loss analysts had expected, but revenue missed the $1.62 billion consensus. CNBC noted that the stock has more than doubled since the start of 2023. This article reports market moves only and is not investment advice.

How big is the launch expected to be?

CNBC's November 14 report puts the business stakes plainly. Sensor Tower estimated more than 12 million preorders through November 13. Morgan Stanley forecasts about 40 million copies sold by March 2027. Roth analyst Eric Handler estimated that Rockstar spent more than $1 billion developing the game. Speaking on CNBC's "Squawk Box," Zelnick said that "our expectations were high and they are being vastly exceeded so far."

The benchmark is Grand Theft Auto V. According to Take-Two's fiscal first-quarter 2027 results, as reported by Outlook Respawn in August 2026, GTA V has sold in more than 230 million units worldwide since its 2013 debut. CNBC notes it is still one of the five most-watched games on Twitch, 13 years after launch.

How much does GTA 6 cost, and when can you play?

Rockstar opened preorders on June 25, 2026. Game Informer reported that the Standard Edition costs $79.99, $10 more than the usual $69.99 for a new console game, and the Ultimate Edition costs $99.99 with extra vehicles, weapons, businesses and other in-game items. The physical version is a code in a box, not a disc.

Engadget reported that Xbox pre-loading began at 7 p.m. EST on November 9 and PS5 pre-loading at midnight local time on November 10. The download is 212GB, so check your storage. Physical copies began shipping on November 12, and their codes also allow pre-loading. According to ComicBook.com, the game unlocks at midnight local time on November 19, so players in some regions get it earlier.

PC players will have to wait. Rockstar has not announced a PC version. Reports that it could arrive in early 2027 have not been confirmed.

How many times was GTA 6 delayed?

The game was delayed twice:

  • Original target: fall 2025.
  • First delay (May 2, 2025): moved to May 26, 2026, as reported by Business Today.
  • Second delay (November 6, 2025): moved to November 19, 2026. Engadget quoted Rockstar: "We are sorry for adding additional time to what we realize has been a long wait."

FAQs

When does GTA 6 come out?

November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It unlocks at midnight local time.

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not at launch. Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a date for one.

How big is the GTA 6 download?

Up to 212GB. Pre-loading has been open since November 9 on Xbox and November 10 on PS5.

Could GTA 6 be delayed again?

That looks very unlikely. Take-Two says development is complete and pre-loads are already live.

Checked on November 16, 2026, against Take-Two's SEC-filed Q2 FY2027 earnings release, CNBC, The Motley Fool, Game Informer, Engadget, ComicBook.com, Beebom, Business Today and Outlook Respawn.
