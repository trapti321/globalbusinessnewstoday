Nvidia Names CFO Colette Kress Interim CEO After Jensen Huang Resigns; Board Starts Search

Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO on Nov. 12. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO as the board seeks a successor and shares log a six-week losing streak.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 16, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

Nvidia named Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress interim chief executive on November 12, 2026, after co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO and board member with immediate effect. The board is now looking for a permanent successor, and Nvidia shares just had their longest weekly losing streak since 2012. The company reports quarterly results on Tuesday, November 17.

Nvidia leadership change, the key facts:

  • Departure: Jensen Huang resigned as president, CEO and director on November 12, effective immediately, according to Nvidia's SEC filing.
  • Interim CEO: Colette Kress, CFO since September 2013, leads the company and keeps her finance role.
  • New board chair: Harvey C. Jones, a former Synopsys CEO, becomes Nvidia's first-ever board chair.
  • Succession: The board says it is still choosing a search firm and has not formally engaged any candidates.
  • Stock: Shares fell about 13% for the week, a sixth straight weekly decline, CNBC reported.
  • Next date: Third-quarter fiscal 2027 results arrive November 17, with a call at 2 p.m. PT.

Why Did Jensen Huang Step Down as Nvidia CEO?

Nvidia's announcement did not give a reason. The exit followed news reports about Huang's personal relationships. The New York Times reported that U.S. officials had concluded a businesswoman linked to him was a Chinese intelligence asset, and it also reported a relationship with a senior Nvidia executive. Reuters later reported a relationship with a second employee, CNBC noted.

In a letter to employees, Huang apologized for being "unfaithful to my wife," CNBC reported. The board said it had no reason to believe national security was compromised and opened an independent investigation, Al Jazeera reported. CNBC said the board hired the law firm Wachtell Lipton for that review. House Foreign Affairs Chair Brian Mast said Huang agreed to testify before Congress before Thanksgiving.

Who Is Colette Kress, Nvidia's Interim CEO?

Kress joined Nvidia in September 2013 after finance roles at Cisco, Microsoft and Texas Instruments, according to CNBC. She will keep serving as executive vice president and CFO while she leads the company.

"Our focus remains on serving our customers, partners, developers and shareholders," Kress said in the company's statement. New chair Harvey Jones, who joined the board in 1993, said: "The Board has full confidence in Colette and the leadership team." The board said it expects operations and customer commitments to continue without interruption.

Analyst Dan Ives told CNBC he expects Kress to hold the job for about a year and thinks the permanent pick will likely come from outside the company.

Is Nvidia Considering Arm CEO Rene Haas?

The Financial Times reported on November 13 that Nvidia's board had approached Arm CEO Rene Haas, a former Nvidia executive, as one of several candidates. The FT said there was no guarantee he would be offered the job or accept it. Arm declined to comment, and Reuters said it could not independently verify the report, according to RTE, which carried the Reuters story.

Nvidia's board then said it was selecting a search firm and had not formally engaged any candidates. "Any speculation regarding individual candidates, whether internal or external, is premature," the board said, Reuters reported. Analysts have also mentioned Nvidia's Ian Buck, AMD's Lisa Su and Micron's Sanjay Mehrotra.

Are Employees and Musk Pushing for Huang's Return?

Wired reported that a longtime Nvidia engineer asked the board in an internal post to weigh employee sentiment, and that the post drew more than 1,600 comments, several urging a way to bring Huang back. Elon Musk wrote on X that a petition to reinstate Huang was underway and that he would sign it. It was unclear whether he meant the internal effort, and Nvidia declined to comment on either, Reuters reported. Nvidia has announced no plan to reinstate Huang.

How Has Nvidia Stock Performed?

Shares fell more than 13% at the open on November 12 before closing down 4.3%, CNBC reported. The weekly slide extended the stock's losing streak to six weeks, the longest since 2012. Nvidia has lost roughly $1.7 trillion in market value, or about 30%, since early October, and it fell below Apple in market value.

Several brokers cut targets. Baird downgraded the stock to neutral and lowered its target from $500 to $200, Cantor Fitzgerald cut its target from $350 to $250, and Stifel moved from $315 to $205, CNBC reported.

What Is Trump's AI "National Champions" List?

The Wall Street Journal reported on November 15 that the Trump administration plans to name more than two dozen U.S. AI companies, including Nvidia, Amazon, Microsoft, OpenAI and Anthropic, as "national champions" to help them expand overseas and compete with Chinese rivals. Reuters said it could not immediately verify the report, according to Yahoo Finance.

For background, read our earlier report on the board's investigation into Huang.

What Happens Next?

Kress faces her first test as interim CEO on November 17, when Nvidia reports results at about 1:20 p.m. PT. Investors will watch for guidance, any update on the CEO search firm, and the board's investigation.

FAQs

Who is running Nvidia now?

CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and keeps her CFO duties. Harvey C. Jones is the board's first-ever chair.

Has Nvidia picked a permanent CEO?

No. The board says it is choosing a search firm, will consider internal and external candidates, and has not formally engaged anyone.

Why did Jensen Huang resign?

Nvidia gave no reason. His exit followed New York Times and Reuters reports about his personal relationships. Huang apologized to employees, and the board opened an independent investigation.

When does Nvidia report earnings?

Nvidia reports third-quarter fiscal 2027 results on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, with a conference call at 2 p.m. PT.
