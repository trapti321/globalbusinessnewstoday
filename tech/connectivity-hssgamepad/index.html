Connectivity HSSGamepad: Is It a Real Brand? Plus Fixes for Controller Connection Problems

Connectivity HSSGamepad problems explained: we found no real HSSGamepad brand. Official Bluetooth, USB and dongle fixes from Microsoft, Sony and Nintendo.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 15, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

"HSSGamepad" does not appear to be a real controller brand. We found no manufacturer, model number, manual, app or firmware under that name; the term shows up only in SEO blog posts, including a 2025-registered website called hssgamepad.com that publishes articles about the keyword but sells nothing. If your controller won't connect, the fix depends on how it connects (Bluetooth, USB or a 2.4GHz dongle) and what you're connecting it to. The steps below come from Microsoft, Sony, Nintendo, Apple, Google and Valve.

Connectivity HSSGamepad, the key facts:

  • Manufacturer: none found. One guide on the term itself calls it a "category" of controllers sold "under various brand names".
  • hssgamepad.com: a WordPress blog registered on July 12, 2025, whose posts call "Connector HSSGamepad" "a good keyword".
  • Apps and code: no app named HSSGamepad on the App Store or Google Play, and 0 GitHub repositories.
  • What to do: identify your controller's real brand and model, then follow the official pairing steps for your device.

What Is HSSGamepad?

As far as we can verify, it is a search keyword rather than a product. The ranking article on electroniclearning.org describes a controller that connects by Bluetooth, USB or a 2.4GHz dongle, but never names who makes it. Its troubleshooting steps are generic and cite no sources.

Other pages contradict each other. One calls HSSGamepad a "category of multi-platform wireless gaming controllers" that do not come "from a single verified manufacturer". Another describes it as a controller-mapping tool for HearthStats, the old Hearthstone stats tracker, while admitting there is "no reliable evidence" it is official software. HearthStats was real, but its GitHub repositories have not been updated since 2017 and none mentions a gamepad.

The domain hssgamepad.com, according to public registration records, was created on July 12, 2025. Its homepage lists only blog posts from October and November 2026 with titles like "Connectivity Issues HSSGamepad" and a contact page. There is no shop, no product page and no support download.

We also checked GitHub, PyPI, npm, Apple's App Store and Google Play for "hssgamepad" and found nothing by that name.

Step 1: Find Out What Controller You Actually Have

Look on the back of the controller, the box or your order page for a brand and model number. Nintendo's own pairing guide starts this way, asking owners to look for the model number "HAC-013" on a Pro Controller, and warns that third-party controllers may need to be replaced with an official one. Once you know the real brand, its support site is the only safe place for apps or firmware. Do not download "HSSGamepad drivers" from random sites.

How to Fix Bluetooth Controller Connection Problems

Most connection failures come down to pairing mode, stale pairings or interference.

DeviceOfficial steps
Windows PCMicrosoft: make sure Bluetooth is on and airplane mode is off, keep the device charged and in range, remove the device and add it again, run the troubleshooter in Get Help and update drivers.
iPhone, iPad, MacApple: put the controller in pairing mode, select it in Bluetooth settings; if it misbehaves, choose "Forget This Device" and pair again, and update software.
AndroidGoogle: turn Bluetooth off and on, restart both devices, make sure the accessory is discoverable, then tap "Pair new device".

Microsoft also notes that "Unshielded USB devices can sometimes interfere with Bluetooth connections", and recommends keeping Bluetooth devices away from USB 3.0 ports.

PlayStation DualSense

Sony's instructions: turn the controller off, then "While pressing and holding the create button, press and hold the PS button until the light bar blinks." Select it in your device's Bluetooth list. You have to pair it separately with each new device.

Xbox Wireless Controller

Xbox Wireless Controllers can be connected to a Windows PC with a USB cable or wirelessly, as explained on Xbox Support. If Bluetooth pairing fails, a USB cable is the simplest fallback. Microsoft's free Xbox Accessories app is on the Microsoft Store.

Nintendo Switch Pro Controller

Nintendo says to install the latest system update, dock the console and connect the controller with the USB cable. If that fails, press the SYNC button once, then try a different USB port, cable or dock, or pair wirelessly from Controllers, then Change Grip and Order. If it won't pair by any method, it needs servicing.

USB and 2.4GHz Dongle Problems

  • Use a data cable. Apple notes that some controllers pair automatically over "a data-and-charging USB cable". A charge-only cable won't carry input.
  • Try another port, ideally directly on the computer rather than through a hub. Nintendo's guide also lists a different port and a different cable as standard checks.
  • Re-pair the dongle using the steps in your controller's own manual. Dongle pairing varies by brand, and we could not find any official HSSGamepad instructions.
  • Reduce interference. Keep the dongle close to the controller with a clear line of sight, and try moving it away from other USB devices if the connection drops.

When Games on Steam Don't See the Controller

Valve's guide for players says to go to Steam, then Settings, then Controller, and "make sure you have the corresponding configuration support boxes checked" for PlayStation, Xbox or generic controllers. Steam Input's supported devices include "Any DirectInput gamepad", which covers many unbranded controllers.

FAQs

Is HSSGamepad a real brand?

We found no evidence of one. There is no manufacturer, model, manual or app under that name, only blog posts about the keyword.

Where can I download HSSGamepad drivers or firmware?

Nowhere official. Use the support site of the controller's real brand, and avoid driver downloads from unknown sites.

Why does my controller keep disconnecting?

Common causes are low battery, distance, interference from USB 3.0 devices and stale pairings. Charge it, move closer, remove the pairing and pair again.

Is HSSGamepad related to HearthStats?

Some blogs say so, but we found nothing in the HearthStats project's code repositories that mentions a gamepad.
