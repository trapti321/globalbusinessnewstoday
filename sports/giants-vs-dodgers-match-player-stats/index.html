Giants vs Dodgers Match Player Stats: Pages' 10th-Inning HR Seals 5-1 Dodgers Win

Giants vs Dodgers match player stats from Sept. 27, 2026: Andy Pages' 3-run HR in the 10th gave LA a 5-1 win and its 100th victory. Full box score.

The Los Angeles Dodgers beat the San Francisco Giants 5-1 in 10 innings on Sunday, September 27, 2026, at Oracle Park, in the last game between the rivals this year. Alex Call doubled home the go-ahead run and Andy Pages hit a three-run homer in the 10th, giving Los Angeles its 100th win and a sweep of the final series.   • Winning pitcher: Alex Vesia (LAD), now 3-1
  • Losing pitcher: Duncan Davitt (SF), now 0-1
  • Home run: Andy Pages (LAD), three-run shot in the 10th, his 23rd of the season
  • Hits and errors: Dodgers 8 hits, 0 errors. Giants 5 hits, 1 error.
  • Season series: Dodgers won 9 of the 13 regular-season games, including the last eight.

How was the game decided?

After the teams traded single runs in the second and fourth innings, the score stayed 1-1 until extra innings. Giants manager Tony Vitello was ejected in the third inning by plate umpire Ryan Blakney for arguing a checked-swing call on Mookie Betts, his major-league-high seventh ejection of the season, according to the Associated Press recap on ESPN.

In the 10th, Call lined a two-out double off Davitt to score Rojas. San Francisco walked Muncy intentionally, and Pages then homered to left for his 23rd of the season. Vesia had pitched a scoreless ninth for the win, and Phillips closed out the 10th.

What did it mean for the standings and the postseason?

The game was the last of the regular season. According to MLB's official 2026 standings, the Dodgers won the NL West at 100-62, nine games ahead of San Diego, while the Giants finished fourth at 65-97, 35 games back. The AP noted the Dodgers' 49-32 road record was the best in the majors.

The Giants did not make the playoffs, so the rivals did not meet in October. MLB's postseason results show the Dodgers' run (Dodgers' score listed first):

FAQs

Who won the last Giants vs Dodgers game?

The Dodgers won 5-1 in 10 innings on September 27, 2026, at Oracle Park. It was the final day of the regular season and completed a three-game sweep.

Who won the 2026 Giants vs Dodgers season series?

The Dodgers, 9-4. The Giants won four of the first five games in April and May, and Los Angeles won the remaining eight.

Who hit the home run in the game?

Andy Pages, a three-run homer off Duncan Davitt in the 10th inning. He finished with one hit and four RBIs, including a sacrifice fly in the fourth.

How did Shohei Ohtani do?

Ohtani went 1 for 3 as the designated hitter before Dalton Rushing replaced him. The AP reported he had come off the injured list (right biceps discomfort) that Wednesday.
