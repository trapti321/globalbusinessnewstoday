Rams vs Seahawks Match Player Stats: Darnold, Smith-Njigba Win NFC Title Game 31-27

Rams vs Seahawks match player stats from Seattle's 31-27 NFC Championship win on Jan 25, 2026: Stafford's 374 yards, Darnold's 3 TDs, next meeting.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 15, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

The most recent Los Angeles Rams vs Seattle Seahawks game was the NFC Championship on Sunday, January 25, 2026, at Lumen Field in Seattle, and the Seahawks won 31-27. Sam Darnold threw three touchdown passes, and Devon Witherspoon broke up a fourth-down pass in the end zone with 4:59 left to stop the Rams' go-ahead drive. Below are the full Rams vs Seahawks match player stats, checked against the ESPN box score and the Fox Sports box score.

Rams vs Seahawks, the key facts:

  • Final score: Seahawks 31, Rams 27
  • Game: NFC Championship (2025 season playoffs)
  • Date and venue: Sunday, January 25, 2026, Lumen Field, Seattle. Attendance 68,773.
  • Top passer: Matthew Stafford (LAR), 22 of 35 for 374 yards and 3 TD
  • Seattle passer: Sam Darnold, 25 of 36 for 346 yards, 3 TD, no turnovers
  • Top receivers: Puka Nacua (LAR) 9 for 165 and 1 TD; Jaxon Smith-Njigba (SEA) 10 for 153 and 1 TD
  • Next meeting: Friday, December 25, 2026, at Lumen Field

Which game is this? The Rams and Seahawks have not played yet in the 2026 regular season. Their first 2026 meeting is on Christmas night, so this article covers the most recent completed game, which was also played in 2026: the NFC Championship. How was the game decided?

The Rams gained 83 more yards but converted only 1 of 8 third downs and lost the turnover battle. Leading 13-10 late in the first half, Los Angeles punted, and Darnold needed 34 seconds to put Seattle ahead for good: a 42-yard catch by Smith-Njigba, then his 14-yard touchdown.

Smith's muffed punt led to Bobo's 17-yard touchdown and a 24-13 lead. Seattle went up 31-20 on Kupp's touchdown, and Nacua answered with a 34-yard score after a taunting penalty on Riq Woolen extended the drive. Neither team scored in the fourth quarter. The Rams' 14-play, 84-yard drive ended with Witherspoon's breakup, and Los Angeles did not get the ball back until 25 seconds remained.

"You can't talk about the game without talking about our quarterback," Seahawks coach Mike Macdonald said. "He shut a lot of people up tonight." Rams coach Sean McVay said: "That was a tough one."

What happened next, and when do they play again?

The win sent Seattle (16-3) to Super Bowl LX at Levi's Stadium, where it beat the New England Patriots 29-13 on February 8, 2026, according to ESPN's scoreboard. The Rams finished 14-6.

In 2026, both teams are 5-4 entering Week 10 in ESPN's standings, behind the 7-1 San Francisco 49ers in the NFC West. The Seahawks visit Las Vegas and the Rams visit Arizona on November 15.
