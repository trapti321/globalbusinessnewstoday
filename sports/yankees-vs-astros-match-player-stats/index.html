Yankees vs Astros Match Player Stats: Walker, Pena Homer as Houston Beats Cole 5-1

Yankees vs Astros match player stats from Houston's 5-1 win on Aug. 27, 2026: batting, pitching, line score and how the season series ended 3-3.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 17, 2026 • 6 min read
Share

The Houston Astros beat the New York Yankees 5-1 on Thursday, August 27, 2026, at Yankee Stadium, in the most recent game between the two teams. Christian Walker went 3 for 4 with a two-run homer off Gerrit Cole, Jeremy Pena added a solo shot, and four Houston pitchers held New York to five hits. Below are the full Yankees vs Astros match player stats, checked against the ESPN box score and the official MLB.com game data.

Yankees vs Astros, the key facts:

  • Final score: Astros 5, Yankees 1
  • Date and venue: Thursday, August 27, 2026, Yankee Stadium, Bronx, New York. Attendance 41,203. Time of game 2:37.
  • Winning pitcher: Hayden Wesneski (HOU), now 4-1
  • Losing pitcher: Gerrit Cole (NYY), now 7-7
  • Home runs: Christian Walker (2-run, 6th) and Jeremy Pena (solo, 7th), both Houston
  • Hits and errors: Astros 9 hits, 0 errors. Yankees 5 hits, 2 errors.
  • Result: Houston won the three-game series 2-1. The 2026 season series finished 3-3.

Which game is this? Some pages ranking for this search, such as Buzzsport, cover the April 24 to 26 series in Houston. This article covers the newer August game, the last time the teams met in 2026. They did not meet in the postseason: Houston was knocked out in the Wild Card round and New York in the Division Series (details below). Those October exits, and a 3-3 season split, are why fans are looking back at this matchup now. All six 2026 results are in the table near the end.

Wesneski left after a runner reached in the sixth, and that runner (Rice) scored off Okert, so the run was charged to Wesneski. Houston's staff struck out 11 Yankees. Topa's run was unearned because of the two errors in the eighth.

How was the game decided?

Cole kept it close for five innings, allowing only Paredes' RBI single in the third. In the sixth he walked Varsho, his only walk of the night, and Walker hit a 1-0 curveball for a two-run homer and a 3-0 lead, the Associated Press reported in its recap on ESPN.

New York answered only with Bellinger's sacrifice fly. Pena homered off De los Santos in the seventh, and the Yankees' defense gave away the fifth run in the eighth when Diaz's grounder deflected off Lombard's glove and Caballero threw wildly to first. The AP noted Lombard became the first Yankees shortstop since Roger Peckinpaugh in 1915 to make an error in six straight games.

What did it mean for the standings and the postseason?

After the game Houston was 67-67 and led the AL West by one game over Texas, while the Yankees were 75-58, four games behind first-place Tampa Bay in the AL East, the AP reported.

Both teams made the playoffs. According to MLB's official 2026 standings, the Astros won the AL West at 81-81 and the Yankees finished 93-68, second in the AL East. MLB's postseason schedule shows:

FAQs

Who won the last Yankees vs Astros game?

The Astros won 5-1 on August 27, 2026, at Yankee Stadium. It was the final regular-season meeting, and the teams did not face each other in the 2026 postseason.

Who won the 2026 Yankees vs Astros season series?

Neither. Each team won three of the six games. New York took two of three in Houston in April, and Houston took two of three in New York in August.

How did Gerrit Cole pitch against the Astros?

Cole threw six innings and allowed three runs on six hits with one walk and five strikeouts, on 79 pitches. He took the loss, falling to 7-7.

Who hit home runs in the game?

Christian Walker hit a two-run homer in the sixth and Jeremy Pena a solo homer in the seventh, both for Houston. The Yankees did not homer.
