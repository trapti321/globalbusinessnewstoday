Patriots vs Bengals Match Player Stats: New England Rallies Past Cincinnati 26-20

Patriots vs Bengals match player stats from New England's 26-20 win on Nov 23, 2025: Maye, Henry, Borregales, two pick-sixes and the box score.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 17, 2026 • 5 min read

The New England Patriots beat the Cincinnati Bengals 26-20 on Sunday, November 23, 2025, at Paycor Stadium in Cincinnati, rallying from a 10-0 deficit for their ninth straight win. Drake Maye threw for 294 yards, Hunter Henry had a career-high 115 receiving yards, and Andy Borregales kicked four field goals. Below are the full Patriots vs Bengals match player stats, checked against the ESPN box score and the CBS Sports box score.

Patriots vs Bengals, the key facts:

  • Final score: Patriots 26, Bengals 20
  • Date and venue: Sunday, November 23, 2025 (Week 12), Paycor Stadium, Cincinnati. Attendance 65,659.
  • Top passer: Drake Maye (NE), 22 of 35 for 294 yards, 1 TD, 1 INT
  • Top rusher: Chase Brown (CIN), 19 carries for 107 yards
  • Top receiver: Hunter Henry (NE), 7 catches for 115 yards and 1 TD
  • Defensive scores: one pick-six each, by Geno Stone (CIN) and Marcus Jones (NE)
  • Records after the game: Patriots 10-2, Bengals 3-8

Which game is this? It is the most recent Patriots vs Bengals game, and the same game the Buzzsport page for this search covers. The teams are not scheduled to meet in the 2026 season, per ESPN's Patriots schedule. Both are in the news this month for different reasons: as of November 17, 2026, Cincinnati is 7-2 and second in the AFC after a 38-18 win over Pittsburgh on November 15, while New England is 5-5 after a 35-10 loss to Detroit in Munich the same day, according to ESPN.

How was the game decided?

Cincinnati led 10-0 just 41 seconds into the second quarter after Stone intercepted Maye and scored. Maye answered on the next drive with a 28-yard touchdown to a wide-open Henry, and Jones then jumped a Flacco pass in the flat on the Bengals' following series for a 14-10 Patriots lead. "I was just watching the quarterback's eyes," Jones said.

New England did not score an offensive touchdown after that, going 0 of 2 in the red zone, but Borregales kept adding field goals. McPherson's 63-yarder at the end of the first half was the first field goal of 60 yards or more in Bengals history, the AP reported.

Tinsley's 17-yard touchdown with 4:40 left cut the lead to 23-20. Borregales answered with a 52-yarder with 1:51 remaining, and Flacco's fourth-down pass for Gesicki from the New England 26 fell incomplete with 18 seconds left. The Patriots won despite losing left guard Jared Wilson and left tackle Will Campbell to injuries during the game.

What did it mean, and where do both teams stand now?

New England became the first NFL team to reach 10 wins in 2025 and improved to 6-0 on the road. The Bengals dropped to 3-8, with eight losses in nine games since Burrow was hurt, according to the AP.

As of ESPN's team pages on November 17, 2026, the Bengals are 7-2 and the Patriots are 5-5 (second in the AFC East).

  • Patriots: have a bye in Week 11, then play at the Los Angeles Chargers on Sunday night, November 29, 2026, and host Buffalo on December 6.
  • Bengals: at Washington on Monday, November 23, 2026, then host New Orleans on November 29.

FAQs

Who won the last Patriots vs Bengals game?

The Patriots won 26-20 at Paycor Stadium on November 23, 2025. New England has won the last two meetings, both in Cincinnati.

Who was the best player in Patriots vs Bengals?

Hunter Henry had 7 catches for a career-high 115 yards and a touchdown. Andy Borregales made all four field goal tries, and Marcus Jones scored on an interception return.

Did Joe Burrow or Ja'Marr Chase play?

No. Burrow was still out with turf toe, and Chase was suspended for one game. Joe Flacco started at quarterback.

How long was Evan McPherson's field goal against the Patriots?

63 yards, on the final play of the first half. It was a Bengals team record.
