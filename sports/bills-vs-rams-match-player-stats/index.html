Bills vs Rams Match Player Stats: Allen, Nacua Lead Wild 37-34 Overtime Finish (2026)

Bills vs Rams match player stats from Buffalo's 37-34 OT win on Oct 12, 2026: Allen's 121 rush yards, Nacua's 17 catches, Stafford's 383 yards.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 15, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

The Buffalo Bills beat the Los Angeles Rams 37-34 in overtime on Monday, October 12, 2026, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California. Tyler Bass won it with a 45-yard field goal with 1:55 left in overtime, moments after Greg Rousseau blocked a Rams field goal try. Below are the full Bills vs Rams match player stats, checked against the ESPN box score and the Fox Sports box score.

Bills vs Rams, the key facts:

  • Final score: Bills 37, Rams 34 (OT)
  • Date and venue: Monday, October 12, 2026 (Week 5), SoFi Stadium, Inglewood, California. Attendance 73,882.
  • Top passer: Matthew Stafford (LAR), 40 of 54 for 383 yards and 3 TD
  • Top rusher: Josh Allen (BUF), 10 carries for 121 yards and 1 TD
  • Top receiver: Puka Nacua (LAR), a career-high 17 catches for 138 yards and 1 TD
  • Deciding play: Greg Rousseau's blocked field goal in overtime, then Bass's 45-yard winner
  • Records after the game: Bills 4-1, Rams 2-3

Which game is this? Some pages ranking for this search still describe the Rams' 44-42 win over Buffalo on December 8, 2024. This article covers the newer meeting, the October 2026 Monday night game, because it is the most recent Bills vs Rams game.

How was the game decided?

The lead changed hands six straight times, the AP noted. Los Angeles won the turnover battle 2-0 and held the ball almost eight minutes longer, but lost on special teams. Mevis missed both of his field goal attempts, and the Rams also failed a two-point try.

Buffalo opened overtime with a drive, but Landman punched the ball out from Cook and Donald recovered. Stafford moved the Rams to the Buffalo 30 before Rousseau got a hand on Mevis's kick. Allen then scrambled for 19 and 13 yards to set up Bass.

"The kicking game, with our PAT, field goal (unit), not even close to good enough," Rams coach Sean McVay said. "It cost us the game tonight." Allen said: "That's the beautiful thing about football: You get another drive."

What did it mean for the standings, and what is next?

The win moved Buffalo to 4-1 in rookie head coach Joe Brady's first season. The Rams fell to 2-3. Since then, both teams have recovered: ESPN's standings list the Bills at 7-1 and the Rams at 5-4 entering Week 10.

  • Bills: at the New York Jets on November 15, 2026, then Miami at home on November 22, and Kansas City at Highmark Stadium on Thanksgiving night, November 26.
  • Rams: at Arizona on November 15, then a bye, then Green Bay at SoFi Stadium on Wednesday, November 25.
