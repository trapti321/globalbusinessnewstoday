Lakers vs Orlando Magic Match Player Stats: Kennard's Late 3 Wins It 105-104

Lakers vs Orlando Magic match player stats: Luka Doncic scored 33 and Luke Kennard hit the winning 3 in a 105-104 win on March 21, 2026. Full box score.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 17, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

The Los Angeles Lakers beat the Orlando Magic 105-104 on Saturday, March 21, 2026, at Kia Center in Orlando, in the most recent game between the two teams. Luke Kennard hit a 3-pointer with 0.6 seconds left, Luka Doncic scored 33 points, and LeBron James set the NBA record for regular-season games played. Below are the full Lakers vs Orlando Magic match player stats, checked against the ESPN box score and the FOX Sports box score.

Lakers vs Magic, the key facts:

  • Final score: Lakers 105, Magic 104
  • Date and venue: Saturday, March 21, 2026, Kia Center, Orlando, Florida. Attendance 19,597.
  • Game-winner: Luke Kennard 3-pointer with 0.6 seconds left, from a Marcus Smart inbounds pass
  • Top scorers: Luka Doncic 33 and Austin Reaves 26 (LAL); Paolo Banchero 16 (ORL)
  • Milestone: LeBron James played his 1,612th regular-season game, passing Robert Parish's record
  • Records after the game: Lakers 46-25 (nine straight wins), Magic 38-32 (four straight losses)
  • 2025-26 season series: split 1-1

Which game is this? The teams have not met yet in the 2026-27 season, so the March 21 game is still the latest meeting. Searches have picked up because the Lakers have opened the new season 12-2, and because pages such as Buzzsport rank for the matchup. The next game is on January 14, 2027 (details below).

Orlando shot better from deep and at the line, but the Lakers won the paint by 16 points and forced 18 Orlando turnovers. ESPN lists the Magic as conceding 22 points off turnovers, against 18 for the Lakers.

How was the game decided?

James scored six straight points midway through the third quarter to put the Lakers up eight, but Orlando led by five going into the final minute, according to the Associated Press recap on ESPN. With 4.7 seconds left, Banchero blocked a James shot out of bounds. A replay review found the ball had not gone off James' foot, so the Lakers kept possession.

Trailing by two, Smart inbounded to an open Kennard, whose third 3-pointer of the night gave Los Angeles the lead with 0.6 seconds to go. It was the Lakers' ninth straight win and Orlando's fourth straight loss. The AP also reported that Doncic was called for his 16th technical foul, which carries an automatic one-game suspension unless rescinded. We have not verified whether it was later rescinded.

Where do the Lakers and Magic stand now?

The Lakers finished 2025-26 at 53-29, fourth in the West, beat the Houston Rockets 4-2 in the first round and were swept 4-0 by the Oklahoma City Thunder in the second round. The Magic finished 45-37, came through the play-in as the East's eighth seed and lost their first-round series 4-3 to the Detroit Pistons (see our Pistons vs Magic match player stats).

FAQs

Who won the last Lakers vs Magic game?

The Lakers won 105-104 on March 21, 2026, in Orlando, on Luke Kennard's 3-pointer with 0.6 seconds left. The teams have not played yet in 2026-27.

Who scored the most points in Lakers vs Magic?

Luka Doncic, with 33 points on 12-of-30 shooting plus eight assists. Austin Reaves added 26 and Paolo Banchero led Orlando with 16.

What record did LeBron James set against the Magic?

He played his 1,612th regular-season game, the most in NBA history, passing Robert Parish. He had 12 points, six rebounds, four assists and three steals.

Who won the 2025-26 season series?

Neither team. Orlando won 110-109 in Los Angeles in February, and the Lakers won 105-104 in Orlando in March.
