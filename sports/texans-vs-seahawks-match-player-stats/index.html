Texans vs Seahawks Match Player Stats: Seattle Wins 27-19 on Monday Night Football

Texans vs Seahawks match player stats: Smith-Njigba's 123 yards, Stroud's 23-of-49 night and the full box score from Seattle's 27-19 win.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 17, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

The Seattle Seahawks beat the Houston Texans 27-19 on Monday Night Football on October 20, 2025, at Lumen Field in Seattle. Jaxon Smith-Njigba caught 8 passes for 123 yards and a touchdown, Zach Charbonnet ran for two scores, and Seattle's defense held C.J. Stroud to 23 of 49 passing. Below are the full Texans vs Seahawks match player stats, checked against the ESPN box score and the CBS Sports box score.

Texans vs Seahawks, the key facts:

  • Final score: Seahawks 27, Texans 19
  • Date and venue: Monday, October 20, 2025 (Week 7), Lumen Field, Seattle. Attendance 68,632.
  • Top passer: C.J. Stroud (HOU), 23 of 49 for 229 yards, 1 TD, 1 INT
  • Top rusher: Kenneth Walker III (SEA), 17 carries for 66 yards
  • Top receiver: Jaxon Smith-Njigba (SEA), 8 catches for 123 yards and 1 TD
  • Defensive star: Ernest Jones IV (SEA), game-high 11 tackles and an interception
  • Records after the game: Seahawks 5-2, Texans 2-4

Which game is this? This is the most recent Texans vs Seahawks game. The two teams are not scheduled to meet in 2026: neither team's 2026 schedule on ESPN includes the other, preseason included. Searches for this matchup have stayed steady because Seattle went on to beat New England 29-13 in Super Bowl LX in February 2026, and this Monday night win was part of that title run. How was the game decided?

Seattle committed four turnovers and still won, because Houston could not finish drives. The Texans converted 2 of 15 third downs and scored one touchdown in three red zone trips. Seattle went 3 for 4 in the red zone.

The Seahawks built a 14-0 first-quarter lead. Houston clawed back to 17-12 when Darnold was strip-sacked in his own end zone and Anderson recovered, but the two-point try failed. Myers then kicked a 47-yard field goal and Charbonnet scored from 2 yards out late in the third quarter to make it 27-12. Stroud's 4-yard touchdown to Marks came with only 2:04 left.

"Our defense kept us in the whole game," Stroud said, according to the AP. "Got to help those guys out and score some points somehow, some way." Darnold said of the strip-sack: "At the end of the day, I got to get the ball out of my hands."

What did it mean, and where do both teams stand now?

The win moved Seattle to 5-2 and into a tie atop the NFC West entering its bye, the AP reported. Houston's two-game winning streak ended and it fell to 2-4. Seattle finished that season as champion, beating New England 29-13 in Super Bowl LX, according to NBC Sports' ProFootballTalk.

In 2026, as of ESPN's team pages on November 17, 2026, the Seahawks are 6-4 after a 21-13 win at Las Vegas, and the Texans are 3-6 after a 27-24 loss at Cleveland.

  • Texans: host Indianapolis on Thursday, November 19, 2026, then Baltimore on November 29.
  • Seahawks: bye in Week 11, then at San Francisco on November 29 and Dallas at home on Monday, December 7.
