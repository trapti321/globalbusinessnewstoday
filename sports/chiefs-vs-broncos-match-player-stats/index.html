Chiefs vs Broncos Match Player Stats: Denver Rallies Past Mahomes 28-24 (Nov 1, 2026)

Chiefs vs Broncos match player stats from Denver's 28-24 comeback on Nov 1, 2026: Mahomes' 321 yards, Nix's 2 TDs and the final goal-line stand.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 15, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

The Denver Broncos beat the Kansas City Chiefs 28-24 on Sunday, November 1, 2026, at Empower Field at Mile High in Denver. Denver came back from 24-13 down in the fourth quarter, and Malcolm Roach sacked Patrick Mahomes on fourth-and-goal on the final play. Below are the full Chiefs vs Broncos match player stats, checked against the CBS Sports box score and the ESPN box score.

Chiefs vs Broncos, the key facts:

  • Final score: Broncos 28, Chiefs 24
  • Date and venue: Sunday, November 1, 2026 (Week 8), Empower Field at Mile High, Denver. Attendance 76,831.
  • Top passer: Patrick Mahomes (KC), 30 of 41 for 321 yards and 3 TD
  • Top rusher: Kenneth Walker III (KC), 21 carries for 69 yards
  • Top receiver: Emmett Johnson (KC), 5 catches for 128 yards and a 73-yard TD
  • Game winner: Jonah Coleman 5-yard TD run with 1:56 left
  • Records after the game: Broncos 6-2 (first in the AFC West), Chiefs 5-2

How was the game decided?

Kansas City outgained Denver by 105 yards, but committed 11 penalties for 105 yards and came away empty on two late chances. Denver went three-and-out on all three third-quarter drives, then scored touchdowns on both fourth-quarter possessions.

With about two and a half minutes left and the Chiefs ahead 24-21, Singleton sacked Mahomes for a 6-yard loss, and Butker missed a 54-yard field goal wide right in the rain. Nix hit Coleman for 51 yards on the next play, and Coleman scored from 5 yards out.

Kansas City drove inside the Denver 5. On fourth-and-goal from the 1, Mahomes found Rice for an apparent touchdown, but a false start on right tackle Jaylon Moore wiped it out. On the replay from the 6, Roach and Nik Bonitto reached Mahomes for the final sack.

"Obviously a big win," Broncos coach Sean Payton said after his 200th career victory. "And I'm proud of the grit." Mahomes said: "I wish I had done a better job of getting the ball out, give somebody a chance." The loss left Mahomes 80-3 in his career when leading by double digits in the fourth quarter, the AP reported.

What did it mean for the AFC West, and what is next?

The win put Denver (6-2) in first place in the AFC West, a half-game ahead of Kansas City (5-2), and extended the Broncos' streak in one-score games to 15 over two seasons. It also avenged Denver's 31-10 loss at Arrowhead Stadium in Week 1. Both teams won the following week, and ESPN's standings list Denver at 7-2 and Kansas City at 6-2 entering Week 10. With both meetings played, the season series is tied 1-1.

  • Chiefs: at Atlanta on November 15, Arizona at home on November 22, then at Buffalo on Thanksgiving night, November 26.
  • Broncos: bye in Week 10, then Las Vegas at home on November 22 and at Pittsburgh on November 27.
