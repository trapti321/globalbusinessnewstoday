Lions vs Bengals Match Player Stats: Goff's 3 TDs Power Detroit Past Cincinnati 37-24

Lions vs Bengals match player stats: Goff went 19 of 23, Montgomery scored twice and Chase had 110 yards in Detroit's 37-24 win in Cincinnati.

GBNT Newsroom • Published Nov 17, 2026 • 5 min read
Share

The Detroit Lions beat the Cincinnati Bengals 37-24 on Sunday, October 5, 2025, at Paycor Stadium in Cincinnati. Jared Goff completed 19 of 23 passes for 258 yards and three touchdowns, and David Montgomery ran for a score and threw for another in his hometown. Below are the full Lions vs Bengals match player stats, checked against the ESPN box score and the CBS Sports box score.

Lions vs Bengals, the key facts:

  • Final score: Lions 37, Bengals 24
  • Date and venue: Sunday, October 5, 2025 (Week 5), Paycor Stadium, Cincinnati. Attendance 66,115.
  • Top passer: Jared Goff (DET), 19 of 23 for 258 yards, 3 TD, 0 INT, 153.0 rating
  • Top rusher: David Montgomery (DET), 18 carries for 65 yards and 1 TD, plus a 3-yard TD pass
  • Top receiver: Ja'Marr Chase (CIN), 6 catches for 110 yards and 2 TD
  • Turnovers: Lions defense intercepted Jake Browning three times
  • Records after the game: Lions 4-1, Bengals 2-3

Which game is this? This is the most recent regular-season Lions vs Bengals game, and the one most pages for this search describe. The teams met again on August 13, 2026, in a preseason opener at Paycor Stadium that Cincinnati won 16-14. How was the game decided?

Detroit settled it in the first three quarters. Goff missed on only four throws, the Lions scored touchdowns on 5 of 6 red zone trips, and the defense picked off Browning three times. Detroit led 28-3 after Montgomery's 8-yard run with 5:43 left in the third quarter.

The key trick play came in the second quarter. On second-and-goal at the Cincinnati 3, Montgomery lined up at quarterback in a wildcat look and threw to a wide-open Brock Wright. Montgomery played quarterback at Mt. Healthy High School in Cincinnati, the AP noted.

Cincinnati scored 21 points in the fourth quarter, including Chase's two touchdowns, but Goff's 12-yard pass to TeSlaa and Barnes's late safety closed it out. "It's super meaningful to come back home where it all started for me and being able to show my arm," Montgomery said.

What did it mean, and where do both teams stand now?

The win was Detroit's fourth straight and moved it to 4-1. It was also the Lions' first win over the Bengals since 1992, ending a seven-game Cincinnati winning streak in the series, according to the AP. The Bengals fell to 2-3 with their third straight loss after Burrow's injury.

In 2026, both teams are 7-2 as of ESPN's team pages on November 17, 2026. Detroit beat New England 35-10 on November 15, and Cincinnati beat Pittsburgh 38-18 that evening.

  • Lions: host Tampa Bay on November 22, 2026, then Chicago on Thanksgiving, November 26.
  • Bengals: at Washington on Monday, November 23, 2026, then New Orleans at home on November 29.

Who won the last Lions vs Bengals game?

In the regular season, the Lions won 37-24 on October 5, 2025. The most recent meeting of any kind was a 2026 preseason game on August 13, which the Bengals won 16-14.

Who was the best player in Lions vs Bengals?

Jared Goff went 19 of 23 for 258 yards and three touchdowns. David Montgomery added a rushing and a passing touchdown. For Cincinnati, Ja'Marr Chase had 110 yards and two scores.

Did Joe Burrow play in the Lions vs Bengals game?

No. Jake Browning started while Burrow was out with a toe injury. Browning threw for 251 yards with three touchdowns and three interceptions.
