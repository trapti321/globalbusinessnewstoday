Editorial Standards and Corrections Policy

How Global Business News Today sources, dates and corrects its reporting, separates news from press releases, and handles reader feedback.

Who We Are

Global Business News Today is an independent business news site for executives, founders, investors and small business owners. Our coverage spans World Business, Markets & Finance, Tech & AI, SMEs & Startups, Real Estate. Most stories are published by our editorial desk, GBNT Newsroom.

How We Report

  • Primary sources first. Figures and claims are taken from regulatory filings, central bank statements, official statistics, court records and company announcements, and linked inline so readers can check them.
  • Clear dates. Every article shows when it was published and, where relevant, when it was last updated or when its facts were last checked.
  • Separation of news and promotion. Press releases are published only in the Press Releases section and are labelled as corporate announcements.
  • Corrections. Errors are corrected promptly. Readers can report a mistake through the contact page or by emailing info@globalbusinessnewstoday.com.

Corrections Policy

If we get a fact wrong, we fix it in the article and show the date it was updated. To report an error, email info@globalbusinessnewstoday.com or use our contact page, and include the article link and what needs correcting.

Financial and Health Information

Our reporting on markets, companies and health is for information only. It is not investment, financial, legal or medical advice.

Reader Feedback

We welcome news tips, questions about a story and suggestions. Contact the newsroom at info@globalbusinessnewstoday.com or through the contact page. Companies can send announcements through our press release form.
