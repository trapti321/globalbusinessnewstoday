About Global Business News Today

Who we are, what we cover, how our newsroom works, who is behind the site and how to contact Global Business News Today.

Who We Are

Global Business News Today is an independent, English-language business news publication at globalbusinessnewstoday.com, published by GBNT in the United States. We publish news, explainers and analysis for executives, founders, investors and small business owners, with a focus on facts readers can check for themselves.

What We Cover

  • World Business: Global economy, trade, energy and industrial policy news for business leaders.
  • Markets & Finance: Central bank decisions, interest rates, inflation and financial market analysis.
  • Tech & AI: Enterprise AI adoption, cybersecurity and business technology news.
  • SMEs & Startups: Small business finance, cash flow, venture capital and startup funding news.
  • Health & Wellness: Evidence-based health, fitness, sleep and wellness guides, including wearables and workplace wellbeing.
  • Real Estate: Office markets, commercial property, conversions and real estate finance.

We also review websites that many people search for by name, checking domain records, archive history and published claims so readers can decide whether a site is trustworthy.

Our Newsroom

Stories are researched, written and fact-checked by our editorial desk, GBNT Newsroom. Every article shows its byline, publication date and, when it changes, the date it was updated. We link to primary sources such as company filings, government data and official statements next to the facts they support.

Read our full editorial standards, including our corrections policy and our policy on sponsored content and press releases.

Ownership and Funding

Global Business News Today is published by GBNT, an independent publisher based in the United States. It is not owned by, or affiliated with, any of the companies or websites it reports on.

The site is funded by its owners and by press release publishing. Organisations can submit press releases free of charge or pay for publication. Paid releases are labelled "Paid press release", and all releases appear separately in our Press Releases section. Companies cannot pay to change our news coverage or reviews.

Contact Us

Email info@globalbusinessnewstoday.com or use our contact page for news tips, corrections, partnerships and general questions. Companies can send announcements through our press release form.
